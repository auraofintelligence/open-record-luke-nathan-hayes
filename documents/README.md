## Filename and version notes

The [AUKUS](originals/AUKUS_Public_Inquiry_Submission_Luke_Nathan_Hayes_2026.docx) inventory filename ends in `2026 2.docx`. The located copy ends in `2026.docx`; it matches the existing [UNGA81 library](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/UNGA81-Luke-Hayes/library.html) checksum. It is retained under its located filename. The available evidence does not establish whether the missing suffixed filename was a duplicate download.

The fourth-album file is the requested **50-page “(24 lyrics)” PDF**. A separate 36-page public export was found during the search and was not used in its place. The Postcode-Level Sovereign Compute paper is also retained as its own document, separate from the [shorter Senate submission](supplementary/Senate_AI_and_Data_Centres_Submission_Luke_Nathan_Hayes_2026.docx) linked in the reporting guide.

The Blend [Aura](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/) PDF was recovered from three identical archive exports. The [C-Hour](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/) plan and [Sovereign Space Builder](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/SSB/) Part 2 were recovered from identified, renamed source copies. Their destination names follow the earlier inventory; their located names and checksums remain in the manifest.
