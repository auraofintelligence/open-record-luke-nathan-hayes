# Further original documents: foundations, care, civic systems and the wider horizon

[Open record](../README.md) · [Original document collection](README.md) · [Project Atlas](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/project-atlas/)

**Added 17 October 2026.** These **18 unchanged public source files** extend the original collection: 15 PDFs and three Word documents. They add Luke's earlier motivations and Aura architecture, dementia-care research, community compute, Pacific cooperation, employment and cooperative design, democratic infrastructure and space research.

The files are copied from Luke's existing public repositories and his Luke Catalyst website. Descriptive filenames make the downloads recognisable. Each entry identifies the edition, what it contains and how it connects to the wider work. Permanent source links, original filenames, file sizes, creation context and checksums appear in the [supplementary manifest](supplementary/manifest.json). [SHA256SUMS](supplementary/SHA256SUMS) lists the file hashes with paths relative to the supplementary folder.

[Read or download the original](supplementary/Lukes_2022_Motivation_and_Toolbox.pdf) · PDF · 12 pages · **2022; public website URL in the June 2022 uploads directory**

The opening slides are unusually direct about the person behind the work. Luke lists hopes of being trusted, loved and happy within responsible non-monogamy; becoming healthy, wealthy and free to travel for work and fun; and being appreciated for inspiring Joyful Responsible Abundance. The toolbox connects those motivations to concrete ambitions: an avatar, an Aura, a mind palace, a blockchain-integrated app, a cinematic universe, companies, dedicated servers, music and an art gallery. Freedom to love, travel and create sits beside immediate knowledge, intelligence support and a personalised workshop. It helps explain why software, relationships, livelihood, art and civilisation appear in the same body of work. The visible title says 2022; the PDF's internal title says 'Luke's 2021 Toolbox'. Its metadata records creation on 4 May and modification on 24 May 2022. Both titles are retained in the provenance record.
