Australian Law

Visualizing Australian Statutory Codes (Acts)

How to comprehend the complex mess of Australian Statutory Rule? Simple, use big data!

Build Tables of The Available Data Metrics

Date Proposed, People Involved, House of Reps. Vote, Senate Vote, Date of Royal Assent, Petitions, Time in Development, Title, Target Audience, Number of Times Rejected, Number of Edits, Date of All Edits, Lobbyist Involvement, Corporate Contributors, Development Costs, Statistical Effect on Target Audience, Flow on Effects on Greater Community, Arrests, Convictions, Total Number of Rules, etc.

Ask Questions

Which People have influenced the most rules?
Which Parties have influenced the most rules?
Which Corporations have influenced the most rules?
Which rules have hurt the most people?
Which rules have helped the most people?
Who was responsible for creating those laws?
Which rules cost the most to develop?
Which rules cost the least to develop?
Which rules had the most contributors?
Which rules had the least contributors?
Which rules have had the most changes?
Which rules have had the least changes over time?
How many signatures to change or revoke rules?
Which rules have had the most petitions/signatures against them yet they remain unchanged?

There are SO many insights which could be gained by learning to visualize data rather than looking at it in documents and tables. Start asking the HARD QUESTIONS PEOPLE!

Connect Metrics

Certain metrics are required to answer each question and it may be found that new metrics are required to accurately answer the questions.

Once the metrics have been selected, one then writes a software algorithm to access tables with the metrics required, which also transforms that data into visual graphics, from which observers gain deeper insights.
