NSI Community Events

New Proposals

I am using this page to describe several frameworks to empower island communities to upgrade their local services and produce for all local needs in a sustainable system that requires very little or no money from the local community to establish. It will take effort!

Issues I have observed, plus envisioned plausible and exciting solutions to:

  • lack of entertainment
  • lack of easily identified local opportunities
  • lack of public transportation
  • lack of fish
  • lack of fresh locally grown food
  • lack of access to learning
  • lack of communication
  • lack of local manufacturing with local sand minerals
  • lack of locally produced power
  • too many mosquitoes
  • too many sharks
  • too many foxes
  • too many feral cats and dogs
  • too much pollution
  • too much erosion

All of these issues are public health and safety, and or environmental concerns, but the wonderful thing about all of these issues is that almost every raw material to fix each and every issue, is already on the island. It is the challenge of islanders to arranged the raw materials into the right sequence of manufacturing to solve them all in one process.

Step 1: Analyze the environment into infinite detail using modern scientific tools for DNA Sequencing, Molecular Analysis and Atomic Spectroscopy to fully comprehend all of the natural processes which are occurring locally, as well as why and how they are occurring.

Step 2: Use Computer Assisted Drawing to design ecosystem adjustments which solve the issues without causing a major destructive disturbance in the ecosystem, such as terror, extinction, disease, overpopulation, pollution or complete collapse of the ecosystem.

While the skills and tools are possibly not all on the island to perform these tasks at the present moment in time, we can invite the people with the required skills and access to the required tools, to visit the island for themed events and to share knowledge.

If we construct our invitations in the right sequence of language and inspiring imagery it is possible to attract the worlds leading engineers and scientific researchers to gift the local community with the patterns of learning they have developed. This method of invitation could in part facilitate the shared community database on the WiFi Local Area Network.

1: Events to Upgrade the Community

Themed events can be constructed to facilitate multiple solutions in an ongoing cycle of learning, teaching and the sustainable, full cycle production of power, natural remedies, food, drinks, buildings and goods.

What must we think of to ensure events are safe, fun, memorable, exciting and interactive?

  • Sufficient Planning for Entertainment and Sharing Ideas
  • Sufficient Preparation to Strategically Invite Relevant People
  • Sufficient Ratio of Eco-Friendly Human Waste Recycling Systems
  • Sufficient Planning for Shelter, Safety and Environmental Awareness
  • Sufficient Planning for Transportation, Accommodation and Sustenance
  • Sufficient Training for Event Preparation, Management, Service and Renewal
  • Sufficient Technological Infrastructure to Communicate with and Inspire Guests

Next is a list of “How to” event topics I wish to complete with you:

  1. How to Make Advanced Higher Learning 100% Free Within 12 Months
  2. How to Build an Infinite Future by Embracing the Use of 3D Printers
  3. How to Facilitate Environmental Harmony by Embracing the Idea of Infinity
  4. How to Facilitate Full Spectrum Alchemy on a Sand Island in Moreton Bay
  5. How to Facilitate Global Real Time 100% Participatory Governance
  6. How to Facilitate Near Instant Global and Interstellar Transportation
  7. How to Empower the Human Species to Transcend the Debt System
  8. How to Design Your Consciousness to Become an Eternal Life Force

2: Donations to Upgrade the Community

While it is not absolutely necessary for the local community to donate anything other than their consent for any community upgrade, it will happen faster through donations of time, money and access to resources.

As I see it, the following dot points show one systematic way to approach this:

  • start with the local area network infrastructure costs and a marketing budget
  • build a volunteer system with video training modules and online web presence
  • devise a range of logos, brochures, sound bytes, invitations and merchandise
  • invite specific people and gift a range of discounts for sharing useful information
  • invite specific people and gift a range of discounts for sharing access to tools
  • invite the wider community of Australia to attend at full price + ask for donations
  • invite International Sister Cities to attend at full price + ask for donations
  • gift some 100% free holiday vacations to encourage people to join mailing lists
  • devise a list of pre-event, during event and post-event challenges and prizes

For all of these ideas you will find examples on this website and through inviting school children and university students to win prizes and tickets for completing the technical tasks. If this does not work, we can use the outsourcing websites Fiverr.com Elance.com and Odesk.com to fill the technical requirements with capable people all over the world.

3: The Science of Defining Full Spectrum Alchemy

What is Full Spectrum Alchemy? I define it as science explaining magic, religion, matter, electricity, magnetism, light and consciousness to empower the comprehension of the seen and the unseen to define and manufacture anything.

After 5 years of research and development I am 100% confident that, human beings all around the world have almost accomplished the foundations for Full Spectrum Alchemy and Eternal Life. The next step in this evolution of Earth and Humanity, is to bring this knowledge into one place and then share the complete spectrum virtuously with people and communities, all over the planet.

We are awakening into the Age of Infinity and Responsible Abundance, if we choose to believe that we all deserve to have access to everything our good conscience wishes for.

Balance is the Combination Lock, Unconditional Love is the Key, Love is All That Is.
