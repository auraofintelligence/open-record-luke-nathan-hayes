Key Roles
Welcome to Infinity ∞
All of the full time roles listed on this page will be collaborative and part of the team working together on each others projects. You must be a capable team player to keep a role here with ichooseinfinity.
Apply via: iwanttocollaborate@ichooseinfinity.com
With all specific details you feel are appropriate.
Together we will breathe life into all of the projects on this web site, plus many more we co-create along the way. Each of us will over time, learn each others roles and also share our skills with many new collaborators.
ichooseinfinity is about breaking people out of the box that society has encourage them to hide in. The limits imposed by these boxes are crippling…
So Break Out With
ichooseinfinity.com

Digital Marketing Campaigner:
Design and maintain a complex online marketing system and coordinate user generated collaboration, contests, content creation and distribution. You should have a wealth of knowledge in this topic, either from self study, work experience or tertiary education. We will combine our knowledge and guide the other team members through the feedback loops and split testing systems for data capture, market analysis and system evolution.
Digital Media Team in Building Our Infinite Future / Luke (ourinfinitefuture)
Hybrid Global Event Facilitator:
Do you have a Strong interest in understanding the local universe to mitigate global disasters? Have you had some experience as a large event coordinator? Are you confident that you can understand and then facilitate the event described in the video below?
Digital Recording and Editing Artist:
Surprise me with your creativity while demonstrating strong ethics and deeper meaning. This role will have you creating short form clips and advertisements, training videos, live streaming seminars, documentaries, and augmented reality systems. An eye for detail and fast learning is essential, a love of riddles, puzzles, patterns and codes is highly regarded.
Mobile Phone SMS + MMS Campaigner:
Want to make a living using mobiles and laptops? Are you a fast learner who can quickly and accurately understand and recognise patterns in social behaviour and data sets? Experience is not necessary but if you have experience with mobile phone marketing tell me about, or show me an example. Please read all the pearls in this PearTree, before you apply, if you have not had any experience with mobile marketing.
Mobile Marketing in Luke (chooseinfinity)
Product Distribution System Designer:
Be prepared to manage merchandise and product development, as well as processing and distribution globally. Using fractal principals and data analysis you will strategically select and connect with community facilitators worldwide to co-create a cooperative community group buying and distribution network. People should only apply if they do not get easily overwhelmed by mountains of data, timelines or understanding complex systems.
Products include HD Video Recording Sunglasses, Digital Storage Devices, Event Tickets, Standard Merchandise with World Puzzle Logo-Quotes-Artwork, Affiliate Products, DVD’s of Events and Training plus some of the devices from the drTOP50.
Membership and Crowdfunding Campaigner:
This position requires a confident, optimistic and honest communicator with a strong understanding of body language and the English language. The three focal points for this role are all in preparation for proposing cooperative agreements to facilitate distribution of ichooseinfinity.com
Building the General Membership
Facilitate HNWI and Corporate Donations
