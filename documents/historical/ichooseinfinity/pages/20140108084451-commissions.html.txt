Commissions
So let’s talk money.
While it still boasts majority support, we may as well utilize the monetary system to move ahead wisely.
If you promote ichooseinfinity products and services you will earn commissions while helping connect your community to infinity.
I wish to empower optimistic people who regularly volunteer for community organisations.
Will you make “The Infinity Choice?”
I believe in a 3 tier commission structure for selling tools, products and merchandise.
I believe in empowering people who purchase from ichooseinfinity, to sell up to 10 units of the same product or service they purchase and earn a share of the profits.
Example: a $50 commission = $35 + $10 + $5
The $50 commission allowance is divided up and then distributed between 3 commission levels. As I am the first sales person, “tier 1″ I get $35 dollars if I sell a product with a $50 commission on it. In this case, the remaining $10 + $5 goes into an ichooseinfinity project.
Anyone I sell to, now called “tier 2″ is then empowered to sell the same product to “tier 3″ and so “tier 2″ earns $35 per sale, while I, “tier 1″ get $10 for each of the “tier 2″ sales, and $5 goes into an ichooseinfinity project.
When “tier 3″ sells product to ”tier 4″ empowering “tier 4″ to sell the product, “tier 3″ earns $35, while “tier 2″ earns $10 per sale, while I, “tier 1″ get $5 for each of the “tier 3″ sales, and nothing goes into an ichooseinfinity project.
Any sales made by “tier 4″ will see the commission pattern repeat, but the “tier 1″ sales person does not earn a commission and the 3 commission “tiers” move down the line, each time a new level is created. This action, transforms the pattern from what many people recognise as a pyramid scheme structure, into a fair, short term residual income.
It is my goal to encourage community organisations who take up this offer to collaborate, to utilize the soon to be released, ichooseinfinity.com crowdfunding platform. So we help you raise money via donations as well as commissions, and also give you a coherent voice on a simple but effective new platform.
Using fractions a commission would be divided up like this:
Example: a whole commission = 7/10 + 2/10 + 1/10
Use fractions to work out % of different commissions
In a perfect world, if EVERY person who buys a token, then sells 10 tokens which include a $50 commission allowance, this commission structure would earn each person:
$350 from tier 1 direct sales
+ $1000 from tier 2 sales made by the first customers
+ $5000 from tier 3 sales made by the 2nd customers
The income will flow in over a period of weeks to months as the sales flow through the community. Then communication through this network can decide on future products to flow through the new community network.
It is my wish to share this system with charities and community facilitators to bring in extra income to those who are already doing the most widespread good work in the community.
All of the existing honest and caring community facilitators should jump on board first, to ensure that the income flows through their community cooperative network and not through big corporations or governments.
