Participatory Society
What is a Society?
A group of people joined by mutual consent to deliberate determine and act towards a common goal.
What is a Participatory Society?
A Participatory Society is a system in which all of the members are connected together by an information distribution network to empower ongoing participation by all members.
A member is able to participate in all decision making processes which have relevance for the individual member, and representative governance is generally discouraged.
I also describe it as a Decentralized, Fractal, Networked Self Governance Framework which imitates the observable patterns of energy throughout the Universe.
This next video was created by a group of people identifying themselves as I.O.P.S – International Organisation for a Participatory Society. It is how they define a participatory society in their ideal format.
I am a member of I.O.P.S but their envisioned version of a participatory society above, and my personal objectives are slightly different. I don’t believe that writing a formal document or code is useful until at least 2/3 of any target community is connected and participating in the conversation to establish the rule collaboratively.
A simple and functional framework for collaborative organisation is essential for any participatory society to function to any significant degree of efficiency. Without the information distribution and deliberations framework, the best one can hope for is a similar outcome to The Zeitgeist Movement, THRIVE, Occupy, or the Arab Spring.
That is my assumption, extrapolating observations of recent global anti-system movements.
Late 2014 has seen many of the people in Thailand repeating the same pattern with a bit better organization than what I have observed in other national or regional uprisings.
