Solar Maximum Global Disaster Resilience
Be Prepared,
Have No Fear.
This video explains the solar maximum and cycle
It is probable that the sun will one day shut down most of our modern technological societies with just hours to days notice. An event of this nature occurred in 1859 and is known as the Carrington Event.
This next video explains the Solar Shot that would kill our electricity grid and destroy our satellites
On the other end of the scale, a solar shut down will one day lead to a mini-ice age in weeks to months. This is nothing to fear.
If all satellites were destroyed, if all power grids were overloaded and destroyed, if many fires started, if epic storms and earthquakes redistributed that influx of solar energy around the planet, what would you do?
To avoid worst case scenarios we can decentralize our power and communication networks. If we do not do this, then we must prepare for survival and recovery.
To protect small electrical goods, build a Faraday cage. To protect your community, Choose Infinity.
We MUST Co-Create Global Coherence!
Communication:
We must create a mesh communication network using devices such as mesh potato and LED line of sight Li-Fi. These devices will cost very little, and with the right planning and ongoing transparent conversation, we grow a resilient communication network we all own.
We can also use video recording camera glasses to record and share important snapshot perspectives.
If you follow this link you will learn about the camera glasses AND another part of growing a participatory society information framework.
Sustenance:
How would you get your food if the system collapsed? We must connect our communities together if we wish to avoid critical famine and water shortages! A week is one thing, but months? Years?
Connect with your community to create a Cooperative GROUP BUYING NETWORK. This will work for a dual purpose, first the community then controls the prices, and second the community is connected to deliberate and collaboratively make decisions about food supply.
Power:
Decentralizing the power grid is absolutely essential! This can be achieved easily with solar concentration devices and vacuum devices such as plasma dilution maggrav generators.
Maggrav Generators will also facilitate a near instant global transport and logistical network by 2018 and interstellar travel within 20 years at best estimate.
Nations fear this technology, because it spells the end of war and the game of nation states. Adopt this new technology and you are embracing the infinite. By embracing the infinite, all of the problems you know, will melt away as we all co-create Our Infinite Future.
