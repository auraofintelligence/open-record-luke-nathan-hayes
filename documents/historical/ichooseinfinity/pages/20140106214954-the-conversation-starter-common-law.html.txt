What is Common Law?
Common Law is the law of the people. On Earth, it sits below Universal Law as the second most powerful law. It is More Powerful than Statuory Law. Under common law, all citizens, including the highest-ranking officials of the government, are subject to the same set of laws, and the exercise of government power is limited by those laws.
