People who do buy a set of camera glasses, or other tools for decentralizing the power monopolies of government and corporations, will be empowered to sell these tools and memberships to others via sales cards with QR barcodes which will be shipped with each item or can be printed. This will facilitate mobile device purchases on the spot plus each set of sales cards will also have a UNIQUE SALES CODE, in the form of letters and numbers.
The Unique Sales Codes will empower a visualization of how the community is connecting, AND it will be a strong basis for growing a brand new cooperative social model, together, as a community. Yes, you read correctly. We can build a participatory society by passing out uniquely coded cards, posting an item, visualizing the overlay of codes and delivery addresses, and then empowering all members to share perspectives through the network.
We can GROW a Participatory Society! With an organically grown information distribution and collaboration network, and a two thirds majority of any community, it is possible for the society to resume control of LAW from the Government or Sovereign. This will be freely available to all people who have purchased a pair of glasses or anyone who registers as a member of an ichooseinfinity Community Hive/Hub.
If you want an INFINITE FUTURE, or at least something better that the Status Quo, Connect and make the Infinity Choice. Together, as a coherent community in conversation, it is a simple process to remove the power from all of the major distribution networks in order:
1: Communication - Technology empowers cooperative mesh communication networks
2: Government - Participatory Society with Camera Glasses and communication
3: Food – When we come together as a group buying network we can cut out supermarkets
4: Insurance – As a group we can insure the whole community by participating in society
5: Power – Solar PhotoVoltaics, Solar Concentrator, Plasma Dilution MagGrav Generator
6: Disaster Resilience - This comes from successfully decentralizing the other 5 networks
7: The Monetary System – THE BIG ONE, Replace this and we can solve EVERY ISSUE – BAM!
