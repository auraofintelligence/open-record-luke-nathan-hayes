James Peet MBRC

Email to James Peet, IT Manager of Moreton Bay Regional Council 23/01/2014:

Sorry James,
I only just opened your email. I guess you are in meetings now, so I will try to provide some more clarity.
Firstly I do not consent to this communication being private. I reserve the right to distribute my communications with whomever, wherever, whenever, however I please. If you wish to keep secrets then our communication must end here. In anticipation of your acceptance of this term, I shall share with you more information.
I have almost finished creating a pattern of learning, to empower a coherent whole of community conversation. The Next Great Conversation will use permanent and temporary installations, of digital technology designed for opinion gathering, quantifying. and visualizing. The pathway of the data visualization will empower the whole community to make informed decisions to co-define the new rules of society to transition the workforce and social systems towards a co-defined future based on the concept of infinite sustainability
Please read the previous paragraph again.
There will be a 6 to 9 month transition process for each community which participates, where people of lower awareness and life experience are quickly raised up through systemized questions and videos. I call this the “infinity tide.” By 9 months there will be sufficient data and coherent conversation between more than 70% of each participating community. At that point there is no requirement for a referendum because under common law 2/3 majority rules and if there is a near real time communication network connecting more than 70% of the population then there is no requirement for a traditional referendum and more importantly there is no reason for protests because everyone will know how the community feels, and everyone can have a voice.
The cost of installation can be estimated at $5,000 per basic and $50,000 for delux:
$2.50 per person for 1 basic installation to be used by 2000 people
$25 per person for 1 delux installation to be used by 2000 people
$5 per person for 1 basic installation to be used by 1000 people
$50 per person for 1 delux installation to be used by 1000 people
$25 per person for 1 basic installation to be used by 200 people
$250 per person for 1 delux installation to be used by 200 people
For Moreton Bay Council Region I estimate cost of development at between $1M and $100M depending on the combinations of technology used in the build.
For Brisbane Council Region I estimate cost of development at between $5.5M and $550M depending on the combinations of technology used in the build.
For Whole of Australia Installation I estimate cost of development at between $57.5M and $5.75B depending on the combinations of technology used in the build.
So what is in the installation?
A prototype has not yet been built or comprehensively designed, but I know of all the components and it is easy to finish the design. I require 3 months income x $1500 = $21,000 + $30,000 for 3 basic installation prototypes. The price of installations will drop to $5,000 per basic after the three prototypes have empowered refinement of the instructions for individuals and small groups in each community to develop their own local hub.
None of the data collected will go directly anywhere until directed by the participant. Questions answered by the participants will direct information flow, ownership and storage. Its starts local and it stays local if the locals want it to. If they want to connect to other communities to learn more, then participants will share their answers with surrounding communities via a community run cloud that will be established in a mesh network that is completely decentralized.
Perhaps a community center or an open technology display house could house the servers for local residents where they can access them 24/7/365 would be my suggestion. Like mini versions of the Library HUB in households, connected to topic based digital information to facilitate opportunities for co-defining the workforce transition to support an infinitely sustainable future.
The Basic Booth Installation will be 2.5m x 1.5m and it will contain a collection of cameras, pico projectors, microphones, Wi-Fi hotspot, and several small computers. In each booth there will be 2 places for a person to stand in front of two nearly identical systems. The difference, one system is for providing anonymous opinions, one system is for providing recorded opinions.
Another option is more like receptionists who interpret the participants perspectives and answers to the questions and then enter the information into the on site database.
The process will grow in an infinite volution from simple questions to define the perspectives of participants as life is now, towards how they wish life was, inclusive of the concept of an “Infinitely Sustainable Future.” The questions themselves, and the features of the booths will be added to and edited by the community as wikipedia articles are, and like open source software so successfully evolves.
Some examples of question topics include:
Local Community: Urgency/Importance/Target Audience/Environment/Skills to Share/Content Moderation
Regional Community: Sharing/Resource Management/Information Ownership+Storage+Access
Global Community: Sharing/Resource Management/Travel/Immigration/Local Universal Observations+Forecasts
Social Structure: Coherence/Collaboration/Conversation/Science/Research/Experiments/Insights/Systems/Technology/Transition
I’ll be spending a fair bit of time at Bris 31 in the next few months after a friendly meeting with some of their Volunteers and staff yesterday. So if council supports this action I can give you some great PR. Better to keep all this in good light for public discussion
As I am freelancing and I do not consent to the constitution or to representation, I am looking at many ways to fund this initiative. I believe that local council is by far more appropriate collaborators than either State or Federal representatives but if council is not interested then a crowdfunding campaign will begin and I will commence seeking direct donations and volunteers. After 12 months without income or savings, I am not willing to consent to the rules of the code for 2 main reasons.
1: I did not participate in establishing them
2: The system hurts the majority of people and the environment, and the errors are written in the statutory code.
I also challenge you to fund a research team to complete this analysis of Australian Statutory Code
http://ichooseinfinity.com/australian-law/
Then you will see where the biggest errors are and so will the community.
I do not wish to see a situation like Thailand or Kiev and yet there are ripples of dissent already showing in the community aimed at all levels of government. This pathway is a way to avoid protests through collaboration to define a new way without creating any more victims or enemies. The tough line of Liberals is too close to marshal law and dictatorship which disgusts the generally happy go lucky people living on the land of Australia.
If we facilitate a coherent, simple and quantifiable way for each person to have a voice in a responsible and respectable method then we transcend the global trend and shine a light to the rest of the world. Especially while Australia is the leader of the G20. Let’s teach the world about PEACE, PATTERNS, AND INFINITY.
I will also be commencing a 2 year course on “Comprehending infinity and defining an infinitely sustainable future.” With the right support I will provide the course for free, if I follow the tradition university model the course will be $22,000 which is 30% less than an equivalent non-government subsidized science course of $33,000 (+/- $3,000).
I have a lot more information I could present, but my digital tools are not able to produce the results I require, and without some extra money, things are slow. When I get $1500/week + access to bris31 equipment, with volunteers, we will establish 2 x 24/7/365 live stream web tv stations that cover ONLY awareness raising information streams which are constructive towards the concept of an infinitely sustainable future.
Next step is multi-platform global hybrid events in disaster resilience (Councilor Julie Greer has met with me in 2012 about this) and other topics that are required for whole system change into infinitely sustainable abundance.
In this system there are infinite pathways, all co-created by all participants defining their ideal lifestyles. The BIG fork in the road is do we make the transition using money? or do we make the transition without money and we revoke the monetary system in the process of co-defining the social transition.
Big changes using small, simple patterns that repeat at each level so that people can understand and participate in all areas of the system which affect them or interest them.
Please take your time to respond to address each aspect of this email. The more definition you acquire the easier it will be to see that it is a completely legitimate concept that could solve all global issues within 2 to 5 years. If you want those issues to be solved.
I sometimes wonder if politicians and governments want the issues solved, but I still hold hope that you are all good people, trying to do your best, and when you see a better way for society to work, you will not defend the present system. After reading more than 50 pieces of the main Australians and Queensland Acts, I know how the system works and so I overlay you all with a visualization that you are really numbers in a system of statutory code that chews up people, creativity and dreams then spits them out into a spiral of depression and illness.
I ask you to collaborate to co-define a new way with the entire community. Starting by supporting me with the resources and networks I require to build the first prototypes and software.
In closing, I do not consent to this communication being private. I reserve the right to distribute my communications with whomever, wherever, whenever, however I please. If you wish to keep secrets then our communication must end here. In anticipation of your acceptance of this term, I shall share with you more information.
It is hard to convey so much information succinctly but at least now you have some comprehension of the scale, detail and time which has already gone into this mastermind. Perhaps we can arrange a meeting in Caboolture.
Sincerely
Luke
