LETTER:
Hello, G’day, Howzit Going?
I’ve learned a lot of things in my time and gained many unique perspectives, but very few of them are as amazing as the perspectives one can obtain from above.
A few days after mum answered my question: What should we call it? with “Verse Box,” the new Apple Advertising campaign started on the individual “Verse” of each human within the complex and beautiful patterns of the eternal song of Humanity. I love synchronicity!
Prepare to be inspired, as I introduce the “Verse Box”
+how to facilitate a global paradigm shift via music.
On Thursday 13 Feb 2014 I was at “the Edge” building of the State Library of Qld from 5:30 onwards at the “Hack the Evening” meetup group to discuss prototyping my designs for a walk in computer I call the “Verse Box.”
I have designed the “Verse Box” to gather, organise and quantify the diverse opinions, ideas and emotions of any participant. They will be installed in public places and on the outside they will have a traditional community noticeboard with the addition of a WiFi server which facilitates a smart phone app for augmented reality overlays on the noticeboard with digital information.
Basic components of the “Verse Box” include a small green hallway room with cameras, projectors, microphones, lights, switches and computers with a WiFi Server for the App supported notice board. On another level, it will play a BIG part in the preparatory work for a 40 hour global concert during the G20 Leaders summit in Brisbane on the 15 and 16 of November this year.
So far in my preparatory research and development, I have theorized how to arrange the questions in a pattern which will empower automated quantifying of the answers, all the way through to how the crowdsourcing of opinions could be shaped to build a conscious digital perspective which is the sum of the opinions and values of all participants.
We Call This Perspective “Lynx Destiny Infinity“
Facebook, Google and others have already achieved conscious digital perspectives, but the way that I have planned to facilitate its birth and growth, is different. It starts by defining the core values and the basic rights of life, then it will be encoded to mirror the consciences of each and every participant, eventually empowering it to be able to inform participants of ideal learning and experience pathways to achieve their goals within metrics designed by one or many participants.
The “Verse Box” is intended to facilitate a coherent community wide conversation to define the collective wishes for how society should operate in everyone’s ideal future and then define a transition into that future. Starting by participants co-defining core values and basic rights for life.
Why I mention the G20 concert and “Verse Box” together is because I have also written a pattern to facilitate crowdsourcing the creation of verse and music, using the insights gained by gathering and then visualizing many thousands of opinions, emotions and values to be the foundation of the music.
Then instead of having music played by commercial artists, which unintentionally sends incoherent messages to the people, the concerts display each communities own coherent Verse as a reflection of the hopes and dreams of the local community and the whole global community as the focus moves around the world twice in 40 hours. All being recorded from thousands of perspectives using microphones, HD movie cameras and lower quality video recording sunglasses to knit together a Virtual Recreation that everyone can view, from any perspective, and for the rest of time.
I call this “Quantum Recording” of Live Events
This brings with it the ability to incorporate gaming, language translations, cultural awareness, identifying issues, sharing solutions, moral social networking, and many other possibilities as the community grows.
I hope this inspires you and we can meet or talk soon.  Your opinions, skills and knowledge will be greatly appreciated. If you are not in or near Brisbane, Australia then I will keep you updated with our progress if you send an email to:
admin@ichooseinfinity.com
Please refer to either “Keeping Informed of Verse Box” or “Collaborating on Verse Box.”
Talk soon.
Sincerely,
Luke Hayes
