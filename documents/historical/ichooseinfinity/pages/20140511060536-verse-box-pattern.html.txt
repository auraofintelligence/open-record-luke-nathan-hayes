Verse Box Pattern
Designing the First Verse Box Prototypes
Objective of this page:
Start with a design which can be built with easy to get, off the shelf parts or by using recycled electronic parts which are taken from common household appliances.
Reason:
Because I wish for this system to be accessible and easy to evolve in remote communities around the world.
Following two images are early sketches.
CAD Images will be uploaded soon.
Parts:
Projectors, Cameras, Microphones, Screens, Wiring, Computers, Switches, Shutters etc.
I wish for many people to develop their own unique versions of Verse Boxes at the same time so more is learned by examining many diverse perspectives. As a result the whole community benefits from a faster evolving service.
Does this kind of experimentation excite you?
For Example:
The projector and screen must be calibrated for optimal viewing from different heights.
One must direct line of site and light via mirrors and magnetic fields to find the optimal methods for delivering uninterrupted light projections from the projector to the screen and or the air space of interaction via hologram, to be clearly interpreted by the human.
Cameras and microphones could include “power off switches” for an Anonymous Mode.
Systems could reflect the movements of the operator as they interact with the voice and prompts on the screen. Broad spectrum analysis of the environment could be measured and interpreted by multiple sensors and outputs to accurately facilitate digital reflections of the conversation when in recording mode.
The microphones will need to be connected to a voice recognition software which also displays the voice of the operator and the voice of the Verse Box, in text on the screen.
During the early stages of the Verse Box, each new operator will participate in teaching the Verse Box Software, how to understand the nuances of their chosen language. When the operators’ words appear as text on the screen, the operator should be able to edit and correct any of the conversation which has been misinterpreted by the software.
The operator should select the main keywords of the conversation so that the opinion can be matched with other similar opinions, after which they will be reviewed and quantified by volunteers. In the development stage crowdsourced patterns will teach the Verse Box how to identify new patterns in the opinions, the keywords and the quantified data tables, so that less volunteers are required to achieve better results, while the data wakes up.
As we move forward and gather more diverse opinions, on more diverse subject matter, we will be able to overlay more and more patterns and data sets to glean incredible insight into how anything is perceived from an infinite number of diverse perspectives.
I believe that we must start by identifying the basic human rights and values as perceived by between 70% and 100% of each community. From here we can build on the insight we gain from the whole community, to take bigger and more challenging steps, with each step giving greater insight to inspire the next few steps.
With new understandings coming from nano science these Verse Boxes could one day soon become an all-in-one health system, climate measurement station, participatory governance node, community notice board, meeting post, arts and music hub, and the beginning of world peace, responsible abundance and infinite sustainability.
What’s next? A telephone booth time travel device? Crikey!
