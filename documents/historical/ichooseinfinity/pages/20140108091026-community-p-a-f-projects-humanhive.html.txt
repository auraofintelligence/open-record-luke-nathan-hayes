Innovation Accelerator and Hi-Tech Human Hive
The above video is a digital artwork by Jason Silva. For a whole playlist of Jason’s Shots of Awe and Philosophical Espresso Click Here.
Proposal:
It is time for the local community center to take a quantum leap forward in capability via technology.
Today, the world is a buzz with exponentially evolving technologies like computers, TV’s, cameras and telephones, morphing into all-in-one mobile smart devices capable of global instant video telepathy and accessing an infinitely expanding store of human knowledge.
These devices are just the start! Already available are 3D printers of varying capacity to print plastics, metals or living cells right down to precisely arranging molecules and atoms. While high tech wearable smart devices such as sunglasses, head wear, contact lenses and clothing, empower real time informing and human point of view perspective recording.
In some places, people are already wearing pendant devices known as “The Sixth Sense” which recognize your speech, your finger point sign language, and also paints the real world with projected images and digital video content it obtains from the internet. While many companies are already utilizing real time interactive global hologram conferencing.
The point I am trying to get across here, is that opportunities have never been available in greater numbers nor diversity. It is EXTREMELY IMPORTANT that Australians and the entire human species becomes aware of the tools and training to solve every problem that we perceive, and then set responsible new challenges which move humanity towards infinity.
I believe that Cooperation, Prizes and Awards are KEY processes which deserve much more focus and development than society gives them presently. It is my wish to develop a number of High-Tech Human Hive Community Center Designs to inspire groups of creative and curious people all around the world to Connect and “Carpe Diem” or “Seize the Day.”
Global Catalyst Joint Venture Opportunities:
I am looking to establish a consignment supply of the technology we require for this “proof of concept” Community Cooperative Innovation Accelerator and Advanced Technology Human Hive.
I am also looking for cooperative groups and investors to evolve this proposal then distribute the ideas around the world. Think of a global hive network to facilitate global cooperative governance.
To connect email: jointventures@ichooseinfinity.com
All technology implemented in the Hives will be QR coded to Trackable Sales Pages for visiting community members to instantly make online purchases of the equipment once they understand how to use all of it’s functions.
For groups of people, a “Wish List” option with a link to a crowdfunding platform will empower more people to access the technology faster. The crowdfunding platform will be hosted on a national sub-domain of ichooseinfinity.com and will be run by contributing virtual members and Facilitators/Catalysts like myself who live in and manage a Hive.
We can easily establish Joint Ventures with industries like Tourism, Community Events, Training, Music, Art, Advanced Sciences, Manufacturing, 3D Printing Rapid Prototyping, Restaurants, Import/Export, Global Networking and so on.
