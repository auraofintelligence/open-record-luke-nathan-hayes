International Travel
For Free Men & Women
The purpose of this article is to work towards the formulation of a standard format for International Travel by responsible, free men and women who do not consent to be ruled by a Nation State.
An important goal of this is the agreement of nation states to support this travel with a systemic approach to making this movement of people fast and simple.
How would you do it?
This is one example of a global agreement however it has the same problems of present representative governance – Click Here to view World Citizen.
We should think about this carefully, and consider the possibility of a near instant global transport network, a global to local universe digital nervous system, and a future where representative governance is replaced by a fractal participatory society framework.
Click Here to view Infinity PRIZE Challenges 1 and 6
If near instant global transport became available, how can we facilitate well managed open global travel and cooperation to co-create our infinite future?
Would Referrals Work?
Could we utilize digital communication for meeting, establishing needs and requests, seeking referrals, then co-creating agreements for travel?
Would developing penpal style of digital and video relationships before travel, empower better holidays and build fruitful global connections?
Should all travelers be incentivized to create these style of relationships pre-travel?
Globally Connected Restaurants and Community Hives could facilitate these conversations and agreements.

The NEXT Great Conversation makes it possible
As more and more communities take control of local common law via the participatory society framework, simple data sets of new common law will be shared with local people and potential visitors.
If the local custom and new common law are available in a simple format then travelers have no excuse for not complying. More importantly, it will be true local law and not false law of a representative government.
