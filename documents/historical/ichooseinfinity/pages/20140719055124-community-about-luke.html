About Luke

Hello, I am Luke.

In 2008 I decided I was not happy with my apathy and ignorance about the future of our planet and human society. I was frustrated that so few people had a voice that was heard, or were making real progress on facilitating a future I could feel happy and excited about. Self Serving models of Capitalism, Corrupt Corporations and Governments were, and still are seriously limiting our chance at a peaceful, infinite future.

So I decided I would transmute my internal feelings into love and respect. Then I dove into the deep end, commencing a completely immersive, self directed learning process which, almost 5 years later, has seen me transform into a global thought leader and catalyst in my own right. I’ll be honest, I did create some debts and bad vibes through this process, but I am now moving into a phase where I can make up for them.

I am presently aware of 1000′s of the past, present and future, global thought leaders in the global mind, whom have solved all of the issues on the planet, in theory or local practice. The recurring problem to date is, none have been able to facilitate the transfer of enough of this awakening knowledge to the masses, in a coherent sequence.

In my case this is partly caused by my inability to continue to consent to, or work in any capacity which supports, “The System”  after I delved into statutory law to uncover more about the governing Australian Society and the Monetary System.

The three main reasons for this are:

1: I do not consent to any crown Sovereign or the Australian Constitution, or the statutory rule of past and present Governments and Law Societies. In fact, I repudiate and revoke all previous actions and documents which at any time, caused consent to such concepts. I consent to an infinite future with fractal, participatory societies where all rule is common law, co-created by each and every member of the society. It is my goal to facilitate a whole of community conversation to empower true common law and end statutory rule.

2: Although I have a strong comprehension of the corporate world by assimilating a vast number of corporate perspectives via the internet and in person seminars, I am fairly inexperienced in a physical corporate setting.

3: I have not had much success when asking for help. So if you want to help, offer, as I will rarely ever ask for help. Especially when it comes to money.

Just becoming aware of how the statutory code pulls the strings of good people to commit horrible and inhumane acts, made me extremely upset. I am still amazed I got to the age of 29 before realizing how bad things really were. My saving grace was the realization that the solution is as simple as everyone, everywhere having a conversation about change.

The Next Great Conversation, the conversation to commence Whole System Change is very close to occurring, and it will evolve all humanity in a flash of love, light and choice.

I believe that I have arranged the puzzle pieces of our reality, and the concept of Whole System Change into such a system, that confident English speaking people can arrive on the website and be attracted to interesting and powerful information. Information that will empower them to better understand the infinite, co-define Our Infinite Future and begin The Age of Infinity.

I choose infinity, and in my definition of an infinite future, humanity has evolved beyond the monetary system. When everyone everywhere is empowered to act on our highest loving excitement, within the bounds of defined core values, human rights, rights for life and responsible abundance, then there is no need for a tool like money.

Could you imagine how humanity would facilitate timely, safe and productive travel if a near instant local to global transport and logistical network became available by 2018 and to all people by 2020? Click here to understand my recent thinking on this concept.

Humanity must choose between actively co-creating Our Infinite Future OR continue on with apathy and ignorance, by voting for proxy representatives to make all of the important decisions that we ALL should participate in as members of society.

Democracy + Capitalism = A Disastrous Future on a scale never witnessed!

Participatory Society + Responsible Abundance = Our Infinite Future!

If we continue life as usual, our global environment, the monetary system, our food supply, our water, ourselves, and our local universal environment continues to remain in major energetic flux.
That means more potential for disaster!

I Choose Infinity!
Do You?

 

I wish for Humanity to come altogether through all means of communication to commence the Next Great Conversation. To co-create and evolve a set of advanced communication systems to facilitate a voice for all in a coherent conversation to define a NEW system for society. A society which reflects the true values of humanity, looks after all humanity, and facilitates responsible abundance with eternal life. 

I hope you will share this dream with me and together we can co-define an infinite future.

Sincerely

Luke Nathan:
of the Hayes family.
