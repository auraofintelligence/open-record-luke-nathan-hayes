Hello Potential Brisbane Team Member,
I am Luke Hayes and I wish to offer you the opportunity to actively participate in making the world a better place while creating an income and lifestyle for yourself and others with inspiring people. If you work well as part of the team, and you maintain yourself as a fair, moral, empathetic, respectful and responsible human perspective,  then responsible abundance is in your near future.
The following links display relevant information:

http://ichooseinfinity.com/spring-hill-brisbane/

http://ichooseinfinity.com/collaborative-spaces/
This page describes nearly 100 possible revenue streams which I have planned while in my money mindset and they could be commenced very quickly:
http://ichooseinfinity.com/income-streams/
This page contains the Present and Future Projects which links to 20 or so projects I have been evolving. You will also see links to more information about me and my journey:
http://ichooseinfinity.com/community/p-a-f-projects/
I ask that you only work on projects you believe are worthy of your time and energy
This page is the List Directory page where you will access lists to quickly comprehend the scale of the network opportunities that you can collaborate with others to define:
http://ichooseinfinity.com/infinity-lists/
It is my goal to build a team of collaborators to comprehend the ichooseinfinity system. This may take a week to a month, during which time each team member will be asked to recruit 1 person of the opposite sex, with suitable skills, experience and persona to work with them for the next 3 months. We will work best as a team when women and men are on an equal footing with mutual respect and the clear intention of working collaboratively.
Maintaining a steady momentum is the key to us facilitating as many community support roles as possible, a quickly as possible. I love to discover new ideas and create exciting new experiences. I wish to simultaneously facilitate ongoing opportunities for myself and all those who wish share my life experiences, be they in person, via the internet or in video.
Each Thursday evening I aim to be at the Edge building of the State Library of Queensland. There we will co-create the first set of digital campaigns, merchandise, web design and event organising patterns. Click Here for Thursday meetup information.
I wish for 111 full time collaborators plus many more part time collaborators who have:
comprehended the scale of the network and it’s opportunities,
chosen several projects they wish to work on,
chosen a number of roles they would like to learn,
chosen the role they feel they are most skilled and interested to perform first,
defined how much time they are willing to contribute
established their own roster for contributing those hours,
divide roster to 30% learning + 20% teaching + 20% money making + 30% recreation
made a good conscience wish list: health, travel, experiences, learning, gifts, etc.
As we evolve we will organize ourselves into groups, discuss collaborative platforms, understand who is working from home, who is out and about. We will all do our best to align rosters, collect contact details for people we will be working with, collaboratively brainstorm solutions and exchange perspectives.
This is a collaborative organization, much like a cooperative, where each member is one part of the whole and where each of us has one voice of equal worth.
I wish to Denounce the trend of secrecy and manipulation surrounding money.
I wish to move towards a world where Money is replaced by Responsible Abundance.
Are you interested in facilitating an infinitely sustainable future for you and the planet, plus get paid to do so? Email signmeup@ichooseinfinity.com to volunteer for an unpaid trial and you will be one step closer to getting paid to define, and live out your dreams.
I look forward to your response.
Sincerely,
Luke Hayes
