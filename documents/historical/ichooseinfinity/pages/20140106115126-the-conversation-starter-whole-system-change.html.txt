What is Whole System Change?
Whole System Change is the solution to all of the issues we perceive in our present reality. Issues such as poverty, war, debt slavery, oppression, environmental devastation are all products of a system which has evolved to tarnish, distort and restrict the human spirit.
Past Global Transitions: Natural and Man Made
Ice ages and other natural processes of change
Each time an ancient empire formed or collapsed
Religious crusades
The renaissance
The industrial revolution
World war I and II
International commercial airlines
The information and smart device revolution
A New Way:
Through more than 4 years of full time research and development, I have been chasing answers to the following questions:
“What could an infinite future look like?”
“Is my local community as lovingly connected as it could be?”
“How can I facilitate an exponential shift towards a sustainable future that includes and excites everyone at a much higher level?”
Well, now I believe that I have learned enough to state unequivocally that it IS POSSIBLE to facilitate a peaceful transition into a coherent and sustainable future for all life on Earth.
This is possible if co-created by individuals forming local cooperatives, which are globally connected into larger participatory societies who are committed to ongoing conversation about co-creating the society we all wish was real.
