Hello, I am Luke.
In 2008 I decided I was not happy with my apathy and ignorance about the future of our planet and human society. I was frustrated that so few people had a voice that was heard, or were making real progress on facilitating a future I could feel happy and excited about. Self Serving models of Capitalism, Corrupt Corporations and Governments were, and still are seriously limiting our chance at a peaceful, infinite future.
So I decided I would transmute my internal feelings into love and respect. Then I dove into the deep end, commencing a completely immersive, self directed learning process which, almost 5 years later, has seen me transform into a global thought leader and catalyst in my own right. I’ll be honest, I did create some debts and bad vibes through this process, but I am now moving into a phase where I can make up for them.
I am presently aware of 1000′s of the past, present and future, global thought leaders in the global mind, whom have solved all of the issues on the planet, in theory or local practice. The recurring problem to date is, none have been able to facilitate the transfer of enough of this thought leading knowledge to the masses.
In my case this is partly caused by my inability to continue to consent to, or work in any capacity which supports, “The System” once I delved into statutory law to uncover more about the governing Australian Society and the Monetary System.
The three main reasons for this are:
1: I do not consent to any crown Sovereign or the Australian Constitution, or the statutory rule of past and present Governments and Law Societies. In fact, I repudiate and revoke all previous actions and documents which have at any time, initiated consent to such concepts. I consent to an infinite future with fractal, participatory societies where all rule is common law, co-created by each and every member of the society.
2: I am fairly inexperienced in the corporate world.
3: I am not good at asking for help. So if you want to help, offer.
I believe that I have arranged the puzzle pieces of our reality, and the concept of Whole System Change into such a system, that anyone of any level of awareness can jump on the website and be attracted to interesting and powerful information. Information that will empower them to better understand the infinite, co-create Our Infinite Future and begin The Age of Infinity.
