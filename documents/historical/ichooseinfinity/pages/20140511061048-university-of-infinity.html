Comprehending Infinity and Our Infinite Future

For many Australians, a traditional University course that is not government subsidized costs about $2000 per subject in a 2 year full time degree of 16 subjects.

So by the time they have a degree, many Australians owe around $32,000 to the government, or to parents. Unless they have worked smart to fund it themselves or they earn one of very few sponsored scholarships.

Also to consider is the likelihood of gaining full time employment after their course. The levels of graduate unemployment in Australia vary from 18% to 25% around the country depending on the study you read.

Do you think this system is working well?

Consider This…

If you study “How to comprehend infinity & facilitate an infinitely sustainable future.” I will discuss a deal with you that is much fairer than an education loan.

The course is 2 years Full Time and can be completed in person or via the internet from anywhere worldwide.

 

In this course students will be co-creating their future during the course, not after the course. They will also be facilitating a coherent global conversation.
