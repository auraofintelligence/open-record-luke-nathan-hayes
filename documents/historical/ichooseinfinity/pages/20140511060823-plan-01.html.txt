3 Month Goals
Establish The Next Great Conversation in Queensland To Identify Common Community Beliefs And Values
Finish A Proposal To Build A Global Neural Network
Build Web Radio and Live Stream Video Stations
Build An Augmented Reality Marketing System
Build A Non-Representative Governance Platform
Evolve A Training Course For Comprehending Infinity
To achieve all of this I require:
a contact list of >100 people, each with a computer and >20 hrs/week to collaborate
a car (shared or leased or by donation) and a fuel card
A house to live in Spring Hill Brisbane with >2 rooms to establish as recording studios
$34K to $50K of digital equipment (shared or hired or on consignment or by donation)
a personal weekly income of $1500 for 14 weeks = $21,000
Why $1500 dollars? Web Based Subscription Software as a Service. Click Here For Why.
By the end of the 3 months the team and I will also:
have trained all collaborators in many new skills useful in life and business
have created multiple ongoing income streams to hire more collaborators
have created substantial visibility in marketing for all of the crowdfunders
have created multiple digital training systems for the future collaborators
have created a brainstorming community network to build the next stage
have built a suite of 24/7/365 digital community opinion posts (see Verse Box Pattern)
To collaborate or provide support in accessing resources call me on 0413215760. Luke
