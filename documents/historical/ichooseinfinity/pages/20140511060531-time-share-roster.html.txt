Time Share Roster
30% Learning
20% Teaching
30% Recreation
20% Business
How many hours do you wish to work per week?
Divide that number by 10, then multiply by 3 and 2
For Example: 30 hours / 10 = 3, then 3×3 = 9, 3×2 = 6
Which = 9 hours L, 6 hours T, 9 hours R, 6 hours B.
How much do you wish to earn for your time?
Remember that we are establishing a community cooperative so be reasonable.
Do you wish to work alone or as part of a team?
Do you wish to work at home, an office or outside?
Do you wish for a place to access the best technology?
Do you wish to scientifically study yourself + health?
Learning:
Which roles do you wish to learn from this list?
What do you wish to learn about Reality?
What else do you wish to learn?
Teaching:
Which roles from this list can you teach others?
What do you do well enough to teach?
Could it be taught via video?
Recreation:
Which projects on these lists are Recreational to you?
How can you build mailing lists while having fun?
What else is recreational and fun to you?
Business:
How can you turn your answers above, into money?
Which products on this list do you wish to sell?
Which roles on this list are you good at?
Calendar:
Now revise your answers and research them further.
Find mailing lists, meetup groups, new connections.
Then transfer your information into a calendar.
