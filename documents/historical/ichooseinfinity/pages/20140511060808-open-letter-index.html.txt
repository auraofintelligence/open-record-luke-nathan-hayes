Positive Catalysts
A Positive Catalyst is a human being who regularly displays respect, responsibility and love, also acting selflessly to improve the lives of other human beings they connect with.
Elite Athletes
An Elite Athlete is a human being who either; was an elite athlete, is an elite athlete or is in training to become an elite athlete; and as a result is thrust under regular public scrutiny.
Musicians
Musicians are people who encode emotions, sounds and language into stories of rhythm and melody, and when popular, they become a major factor in the foundation of a society.
Teachers
Teachers are people who transfer foundational knowledge of a society to the uninitiated, and engage in a feedback loop with the rigid framework of a society, the statutory code.
Researchers
Researchers are students of life, human beings of any age who are on a path of discovery and definition, those at the forefront of human thought leadership co-define the infinite.
Not For Profits
A Not For Profit is a group of human beings who act as a single fictional corporate entity, usually for the benefit of other less fortunate people by soliciting donations of money.
Wealthy Individuals
A Wealthy Individual is a human being who is born into a family with a lot of money, and/or they have acquired a pattern of perspectives which enables them to make a lot of money.
Large Corporations
A Large Corporation is a group of human beings who act as a single fictional corporate entity, usually for the benefit of themselves and/or wealthy individual investors of money.
Statutory Representatives
of the Commonwealth
The Commonwealth is supposedly a “Society,” which I define as “A group of people joined by mutual consent to deliberate, determine and act towards common goals.” Whereas in truth, the Commonwealth is a system for a minority to seize manipulative control over the majority of the community under the guise that “A Community Can Not Coordinate Itself.“
“Any Community CAN Learn To Coordinate Itself!”
The Commonwealth Is Ending, And Whole Of Community Participation Is Replacing It.
