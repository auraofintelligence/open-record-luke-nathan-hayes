I Choose Infinity
Political Party of Australia
(ICI PPoA)
I believe that Australian Politics has lost the trust of Australians and so I have made the decision to stand up and offer a new choice for governance. The system of democratic governance is coded to imitate WAR, and I am not a warrior. I am a messenger of peace.
I do not consent to the current form of governance in Australia and so I stand to facilitate a transitional government which seeks to solve all known issues in one systemic re-design.
I ask for your consent to deliver a peaceful, responsible and virtuous transition away from economic artificial scarcity, debt slavery, unsustainable practices and secrecy, towards a new era of responsible abundance, virtuous collaboration and participatory governance.
If you do not know what virtues are: Click this link to open the Wikipedia List of Virtues
Maybe you should share the Virtues link with your current Political Representative…
At the time of typing this blog post, I have not registered a political party, although I have downloaded the Party Registration Guide from the Australian Electoral Commission.
The “I Choose Infinity Political Party of Australia” will be registered once it’s membership expands past 500 people, which is the minimum number of members required to register, if there is not a sitting member of parliament in the group.
Following are the Policies I personally stand for:
Free Access to Virtuous Higher Learning Systems, via a Community Commons Database.
All Publicly Funded Educational Institutions be required to build digital reflections of all courses offered in person, which will be 100% free to access via Community Commons.
Free Digital Learning Pathways for Collaboratively Defining Participatory Governance.
Free Digital Communication for All Who Complete a Virtuous Self Reflection Profile.
Focus a Significant Fund Into High Definition Ecosystem and Resource Analysis.
Focus a Significant Fund Into High Concentration Ecosystem Feedback Loops.
Focus a Significant Fund Into MicroBiome Research and Self Doctoring Health Care.
Focus a Significant Fund Into Development of 3D Printing and Full Spectrum Alchemy.
Focus a Significant Fund Into Developing A Near Instant National Transport Network.
Focus a Significant Fund Into Local, Clean, Safe, Sustainable Power Production.
Focus a Significant Fund Into Seeding International Collaboration Towards Peace.
Focus a Significant Fund Into Defining Instant Translation Communication Systems.
Aim for complete definition of a new constitution for Australia and a Referendum for Change on the 160th Anniversary of the Eureka Stockade, December 03, 2014.
I know many other solutions but I have limited resources to share them.
I am happy to share my perspective on topics of national interest upon request.
I have completed much research and development, over more than 5 years, to earn the confidence to say that a sustainable future of responsible abundance is possible. As a matter of fact, it is very close to our current reality, if we are collectively brave enough to define the transition, and our own places within our ideal sustainable future.
It is your responsibility, just as much as it is mine, to participate in defining the transition stage, as well as the sustainable system of Responsible Abundance which will support all life on Earth. I strive to define a system which interests, includes and excites all people in all places to a much higher level of Love, Respect, Responsibility, Confidence, Well Being, Awareness and Ability.
If you do not participate in defining a new system of society, you are selling yourself short.
If you plead “ignorance is bliss” and leave all the decisions to the economists, corporate lawyers and politicians, then you are selling the loving potential of planet Earth short.
If you hoard your resources and choose to not share them with your local community then please consider redefining your purpose in life to experience fulfillment in virtuous ways.
If you wish to participate in the early stage establishment of this political party, please do contact me as soon as possible at luke.chooseinfinity@gmail.com or on 0413215760.
If you have time and/or resources which you are willing to donate as in-kind support, they will be much appreciated.
If you wish to become a foundation member and make a donation, please make an offer and then we will record a digital reflection of our conversation for the public record.
If you wish to do some due diligence on me, Luke Nathan: of the Hayes family, this website is a great place to start. I have a significantly large online history, much of which is linked to via this website. I no longer use my facebook profiles as the website is TOXIC, though I have left them online for people to review my past.
If you wish to volunteer to collaboratively review my complete history (it is very diverse and quite interesting) into a digital reflection and biography, I am excited to make time.
In Closing:
I am not a saint, I have hurt people, I have done things I would not repeat.
I am proud of where I am now, what I have achieved, and the direction I am going.
I have tried many things, had many experiences, visited many places, met many people.
I am a student of Life, trying to survive, improve and thrive in a world of hypocritical rules, sensationalist media and market forces that distort good conscience and common sense.
If you gift your consent, and then I become the Transitional Prime Minister of Australia, I promise to do my best to work with All People of Virtuous Intent to deliver a bright future.
To achieve this goal we require hundreds of people to stand for election into the house of representatives, dozens to stand for the senate and thousands to volunteer their time to support the development of a network throughout all the communities in Australia.
Please stand with me in person, in voice, in digital reflection and by your vote.
Sincerely and with Unconditional Love,
Luke Nathan: of the Hayes family
Born: Redcliffe, Australia December 03, 1982.
Residing: Amity, Minjerribah, Moreton Bay, Australia.

Life as a Puzzle
March 27, 2014Uncategorizedadmin
My life
has been all about
gaining as many perspectives as I can.
At some point in my past I began to view the Earth
and the reality of all of my life experiences as one
great big puzzle made up of infinite perspectives.
In the beginning it was all about fun, the new, defining the infinite unknown but with no guides that were pure, all light distorted by clouded definitions and misaligned values. How to find the truth if all interactions, codes and experiences only contain a twisted truth at best? How to unravel the complexity to discover more definite truth? Think BIGGER.
By thinking bigger, one runs the risk of making bigger mistakes, but this process of mind expansion finds the truth by overlaying competing perspectives in infinite combinations. The truth is out there if you seek to Think BIGGER. Each perspective, a piece of a fractured mirror image of your own self, your own values, your own fears, your own perfect love.
Near the edge of each stage of expansion, I notice a feeling of being overwhelmed by the complexity of the latest layers of new perspectives. This is the signal to inspire and seek patterns in the complexity which lead to the discovery of truth. As each pattern is clearly defined, the next expansion becomes exponentially bigger, faster and more exciting.
What is the core truth? we are all one energy
What is the next truth? we are all perspectives of the one
What is the next truth? reality is reflections of our self perspective
What is the next truth? our core values define the experiences we manifest
What is the next truth? our core values are defined and governed by our conscience
What is the next truth? our conscience is magnetic, in good conscience we attract light, in bad conscience we repel light. Each perspective defines all perspectives in each moment.
