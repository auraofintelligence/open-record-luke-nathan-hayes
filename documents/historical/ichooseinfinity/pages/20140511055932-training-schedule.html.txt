Coordinating Collaborators to Achieve Wealth/Health/Freedom
Formal Qualifications Are NOT Required. Curiosity + Persistence + Patience + Confidence + Respect ARE!
I am planning to record video “role descriptions” for each of the roles below. As I complete each video I will link them to each Title below. After this, new recruits will create subsequent vol∞tions of training videos.
My objective in doing this is to give you all an example of free, creative commons learning systems that can support people to choose their own path rather than be locked in a box like many present education systems and organisations do. Each participant will be encouraged to improve the #ideastreams into advanced, systematic learning pathways.
These pathways will be a combination of weekly video blogs in which we share insights, achievements, observations and even failures. Then during and a major review at least once every 3 months, a team will condense the information into a fast track system for people all over the world to access and share to facilitate global opportunities.
The biggest thing that most organisations fail to understand is that the more you give away for free, the larger your community grows and the faster it grows. If what you are giving away for $0 does facilitate an improvement in peoples’ lives, like learning can do, then the community will trust and participate at a much higher level.
