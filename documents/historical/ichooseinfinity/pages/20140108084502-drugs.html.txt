Drugs Are Consciousness Distorting Substances
Statutory Legislation and Poor Classification of so called “legal” and “illegal” Drugs, is confusing people, harming people and leads to death in too many cases.
The law has locked up much of the useful information.
It is my personal belief that a complete overhaul of all consciousness distorting substances in Australia is required to save lives. There is simply too much left undefined or is not easily accessible.
We could establish local Consciousness Parlours where people can cooperate to facilitate a safe environment for manufacture and experimentation with drugs.
We must get creative, be honest and be responsible when designing these spaces. Aim to inspire exciting projects and challenges, confidence, friendship and set sensible objectives.
Doing this would remove the violence, fear and health dangers so often associated with drugs and alcohol. In most cases it is feasible to assume that many negative behaviours observed in drug users, stem from the fear inducing frame of reference created by law, not due to the drugs themselves.
This video is about a recent paper linking psychedelic mushrooms to snapping people out of depression.
