Comprehending Infinity
I Choose Infinity ∞
On this page I will be showcasing the Evolution of my ideas and beliefs from inception, to pattern, to poem, to song and then into an album with video.
The following playlist contains 7 of the 11 poems that will be on my first album “Comprehending Infinity” to be released worldwide in 2014.
I am doing something completely different to the old school distribution of music. These poems are creative commons, which means I authorize you to use them and evolve them in any way you see fit as long as you credit that you got the original concept from me, and link back to my original content.
I hence forth set you a challenge:
To utilize any or all of my poems to compose your own music in your own unique way. Let’s bring some much needed morals and philosophy back into POP Culture.
In the future, perhaps in my 2nd album and definitely in my 3rd album, I wish to collaborate with the artists who are voted to have created the best adaptations.
Now imagine, define and co-create Our Infinite Future
