List Building 101
The Money Is In The List
Consider for a moment you are not part of a team and you want to build an easy income, you will have to manage your time very well in the beginning, but once you have your list it is smooth sailing. Read the following information and consider how much easier it would be to establish an organisation that works in this way, as part of a team of specialists, even if none of the team have any start-up funds or income.
It’s a Numbers Game:
To earn $1000 per week, selling products with an average of $10 profit per product, how many products would you have to sell to make your weeks earnings? Answer = 100
If you work 5 days per week 8 hours per day you work 40 hours per week. By following the Infinity time share, 30% learning, 20%teaching, 30% recreation, and 20% business, you have 1 day, or 8 hours to earn your $1000. How can you do it? Answer = Build a List
8 hours to sell 100 products with an average profit margin of $10 per product sold. Spend 2 by 4 hours building a twice weekly newsletter with info + products, then email your list.
A 5% conversion of new subscribers = 5 people from every 100 make a purchase.
If 5 by $10 profit = $50 earned per 100 new subscribers. Then $1000 / $50 = 20,
20 x 100 new subscribers = 2000 new subscribers to reach your 1st milestone
continue list building for 6 weeks, aim to have a list of >12,000 subscribers
more subscribers mean more collaborative spaces, tools, and events.
Now you have some targets, you have 4 days to build your list, let’s break that up now into the time share.
20% = 1 day = 8 hours for Teaching
30% = 1.5 days = 12 hours for Learning
30% = 1.5 days = 12 hours for Recreation
Create things to do and build yourself a weekly roster!
12 hr/week Recreation = Art, Music, Events, Out and About, Sport!
12 hr/week Learning = Networking, Researching, Watching Training Videos!
8 hr/week Teaching = Video Recording, Audio Recording, Lecturing, Demonstrating!
Now ask yourself
While Thinking as an Individual and as a Team
How can my artworks and music build the mailing list?
How can hosting events, or a sport build the mailing list?
How can networking or researching build the mailing list?
How can making great training videos build the mailing list?
How can my video and audio recording build the mailing list?
How can my lecturing and demonstrating build the mailing list?
Who is my target audience for each of these activities?
What can I gift them from ichooseinfinity.com to empower them?
What are the ideal ichooseinfinity.com community projects to tell them about?
This System is Scalable, and if we have specialists start each focus area and then record videos to train every other participant, we will all become capable of working each part of the system, very, very quickly.
