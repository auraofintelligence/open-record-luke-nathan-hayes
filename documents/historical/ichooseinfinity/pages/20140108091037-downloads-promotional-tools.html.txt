QR Codes A4 PDF
If you want to help promote ichooseinfinity.com then you can print out a few A4 pages of QR Codes and website URL’s to present to people while you go about your daily routine. At Office Works, it cost me 10 cents per standard A4 paper with black ink.
That means that for $10:
you can get 100 pages of QR Codes printed.
4 codes per page x 100 pages = 400 QR Codes for $10
6 codes per page x 100 pages = 600 QR Codes for $10
12 codes per page x 100 pages = 1,200 QR Codes for $10
24 codes per page x 100 pages = 2,400 QR Codes for $10
or you can print a combination of different codes to suit your target audience.
(it may be more expensive if Office Works Staff do it for you)
Be warned, it took me well over 1 hour to cut out all 2,400 codes out using an office guillotine. I also cut the plastic packaging out of my Asus windows tablet box to make a cool carry container with quick easy access when handing codes out in crowded venues.
