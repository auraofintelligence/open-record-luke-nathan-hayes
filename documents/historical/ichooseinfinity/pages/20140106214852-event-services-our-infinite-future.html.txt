Our Infinite Future
What is an Infinite Future?
An Infinite Future is whatever is co-created by all of us, every person on Earth, some time in the future when we decide to co-create a way to connect everyone, everywhere, to discuss and decide upon the basic foundations for Human Rights.
Then we will co-create a tool for all mankind, to utilize to interact with the universe as the infinitely powerful beings we all are. Free from the suffocating restrictions of yesterday and yesteryear. Imagine how as ONE and as INDIVIDUALS we could learn to understand, develop and maintain the Local Universal Environment and co-craft an exciting universe, with but a distant memory of the fear, persecutions and suffering.
“If you connect to the Next Great Conversation you will discover some infinitely new possibilities and realities.”
How is it Possible?
It is possible if we believe that it is possible. Believe this “if you or any other human being can conceive a thought, then it is possible. It may not be probable, but it is possible.”
An infinite future is possible, through the infinity choice. Choose to connect with your local community, establish many small community hubs or CONNECTION POINTS where community facilitators collaborate to co-create the local Next Great Conversation.
Click to view the Innovation Accelerator Human Hive Community Center Pitch
How Can I Help?
Each community will require many community facilitators, people who create dynamic questionnaires to gain comprehensive understanding of the needs, requests, thoughts, emotions, and life goals of the community so they can assist in connecting the right people together. They will also facilitate community meetings and events which connect the local region in an infinite number of creative and exciting ways.
Click to see an Example of a dynamic 7 part community survey – By Luke Hayes
As each community achieves greater than two thirds (2/3) of the population connecting into the Next Great Conversation, it will retrieve the power to control COMMON LAW from all of the representative governments in that community. With this power comes the ability to co-create NEW COMMON LAW, and ERASE ANY existing man made law that previously claimed or held power over that community. To connect is to claim your freedom.
If you are already a community facilitator, or you want to become a community facilitator, please email: iwanttocollaborate@ichooseinfinity.com
In the subject line place: CF + Your Name + Your Postcode
Include a personal profile or resume + a recent photograph or link an webvideo of you.
I look forward to communicating with you.
