The Advice I Got:
A 39 minute and 32 second video on creating productive press releases.
PR Steps for Generating Customers with Marc Harty kindly supplied by Affilorama
The Plan:
Start with 2 premium press releases/month + more frequent free press releases
PRWeb – use a press release service with keyword links that create back links correctly
Research Keywords with more than 10,000 searches per month on google or youtube
Credibility (market + personal) Truth, Facts and Trust: Use statistics and joint ventures
Hooks: the underdog hero story, strategic controversy, angle for uniqueness, bounce off present bubbles of regional discussion and mention people with large lists of followers
List Articles: top 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 13, 20, 21, 22, 23, 25, 30, 40, 50, 100 lists on relative topics
Call to action: Make several simple calls to action for media + audience (i.e. list + purchase)
Rewards: Ebooks, Articles, Digital Media, Affiliate Products, Services, Membership
Contests: Social Media, Video, Art, Music, AR Orienteering, Journalism, Questionnaires
OK, time to write a few practice press releases.
