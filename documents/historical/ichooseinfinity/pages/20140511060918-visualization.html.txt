Humanizing Data Into The System of Systems
I spent a few hours today thinking about how it would be possible to visualize reality using the exponentially increasing BIG DATA humanities tools now provide.
We already have some amazing tools telling us that our infinite reality is much more dynamic than many scientists and philosophers had imagined.
To gain deeper meaning from the Data we must use new methods to visualize it. Then we will observe, define and analyze new patterns and cycles which effect the Earth and our local environment.
∞ Visualization Model
Essential to this model is the Golden Ratio 1 : 1.618
We require 3 Dimensions of Space
We require 1 Dimension of Time
We require 2 Dimensions of Charge
We require 1 Dimension of Density
We repeat the process for each Fractal Layer which must contain all of the 7 Dimensions listed above
EG: Universe: Galaxy: Solar System: Earth: Human: Atom: Proton: Quark etc.

Then we have to add our Patterns of Observation and we can now consider new Patterns of Technology such as Virtual Reality and Holograms.
To be really comprehensive we can rotate the model on 3 axis as well as through time. When we can do this for each of the Fractal Boost Layers simultaneously we will be able to predict the future, understand the past, and completely understand our environment.

It Is All About Patterns
Yes we live in an infinitely complex universe, and yes our human society is infinitely complex and diverse, and yes each human is infinitely complex and diverse.
But, in each layer of infinite beauty there are patterns that repeat in every layer, and when each individual reaches a point in their lives when they comprehend this, they will open their eyes and discover infinity.
