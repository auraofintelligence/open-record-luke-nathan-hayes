It’s MORE Than A Revolution!
Give some children the gift of an infinite future as congratulations for completing high school this year.
For $1,000 per student, with a min of 4, max of 10, I will give them a 6 day course on whole system change.
Day 1 we will commence with introductions and then I will share information to facilitate a national shift to increase community standard of living.
Day 2 will start with recounting the previous days achievements and solving any issues we encountered. The morning learning session will be understanding body language and various communication methods.
After lunch we will venture out into the community to invite community facilitators and local leaders to an evening meeting which will be live streamed online.
At this meeting we will facilitate a live question and answer show after providing some food for thought.
Day 3 will again start with a re-cap on the previous day to learn and overcome new challenges. After this we will analyse the new data and prepare for a larger scale launch event for The Next Great Conversation.
By Day 4 we will have constructed a mighty sea of data and we will be learning by observing patterns that are emerging. Regular confidence boosting activities will be utilized now to hone on the spot decision making when under pressure facilitating event preparations.
Day 5 Event Set-up followed by Party Time!
Day 6 Clean Up + What Did We Learn?
Take Home – Complete ichooseinfinity information pack on your choice of media storage device. Includes lifetime membership and information stream access.
Plus Full access to ichooseinfinity platforms to create content and connect your local community to infinity.
