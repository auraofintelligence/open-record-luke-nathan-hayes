Global and Space Weather Observations and Predictions
This is my first attempt at making an explanatory video about Earth and Sun weather observations using some of the hi-tech satellites humanity now has sensing our universe.
I will be experimenting with a few different formats in January 2014 but I really do need a new computer with faster graphics and CPU processing to create the best reports I can.
If you are interested in learning about the earth the sun and the universe, and you live in or near Brisbane, Australia, perhaps we can meet up and cooperate to cover more info.
