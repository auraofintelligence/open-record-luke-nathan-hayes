Verse Box
Transforming Your Opinions Into An Open Community Verse
Does your community accurately reflect your values, beliefs, thoughts and opinions in the way it operates?
I believe that representative governance does NOT reflect the values of the community.
How similar or diverse do you think the opinions of each and every human in your community would be?
I believe similarity on personal values and human rights, but wide diversity on most else.
Would you participate in a social experiment to define a new system for society by examining all opinions?
I believe that many people will when they understand the process. YES I will participate.
If a system for whole community conversation was available, and as easy as a phone call or a visit to the corner store, how often would you share your opinion?
I would use it every day, to comment on interesting topics unless I am taking a holiday.
Think of the verse box as a 3m x 1.5m booth which will be a computer system that you walk into and operate with your voice and body movements.
Would you prefer to use an operating system like this, only in a verse box or would you also like to use it on your mobile phone, or home based network as well?
The Verse Box idea is to record, and share opinions on any topic which interests or affects an individual or a community, or the planet. Participants have two main options in how their opinion is recorded:
Anonymous Opinions or Attributed Opinions.
Through the entire process the human operating a Verse Box will be prompted with questions about:
Ownership of information created
Storage of information created
Sharing information created
How to improve the system
I.E: add, remove, re-order questions or prompts
I feel that the patterns should be made available to the public, free of charge, under creative commons licence for any person to access and develop without cost.
The 2 strict conditions = ONE: All development added to your copy must be made available to the Verse Box community free of charge, and TWO: NO Verse Boxes are to ever be used for commercial gains.
I wish to find a way to ensure content and access to a Verse Box never cost a fee I.E. access is never charged as pay per use, or as an ongoing subscription fee, or even as a joining fee.
If you Agree to the terms above, Click Here to go to the Verse Box Pattern
