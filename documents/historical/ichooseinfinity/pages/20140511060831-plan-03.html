Plan 03

Digital Community Notice Board And Opinion Boxes

Utilizing SWS Server Wi-Fi Hotspot + multiple video cameras + a pico projector + rasberry pi + Broadband internet connection and some building materials, we could co-create permanent digital access points in the community, for the community to voice their opinion.

These access points would be like a green hallway segmented into sections large enough for 2 people to stand side by side. Green like a movie studio green screen so that one can layer their own content over still images or videos to portray deeper meaning.

In each room people would interact with an operating system via voice, and interacting with projected light images. Once connected, your smart phone or laptop could also use the free local wi-fi hotspot network.

Click Here to View the Latest Pattern of the Opinion Gathering Box “The Verse Box”

In a community of 400,000 people, it would take each person 15 minutes to register a start-up video profile. That equates to 6,000,000 minutes or 100,000 hours if 100% of the community participated.

If 100 opinion boxes were in the community, and the community moves through them registering at a rate of 2 per hour, 24 hours per day 7 days per week. How long will it take the community to be 70% registered?

= 4,800 people per day
70% = 280,000
58 days 8 hours

Once everyone is registered the speed and complexity of the  conversation will continue to increase as we all learn how to interact with and evolve the system, our interface methods, and our preferences for information regarding what interests or effects each individual.

100 Opinion Boxes Could Range In Cost From:

$300,000 to $5,000,000
Depending on Features Desired by Residents

That equates to between $3,000 and $50,000 each, if ordered in bulk to reduce the cost of technology used.

Click Here to see a larger system Proposal for a location in Spring Hill, Brisbane

Once people are connected they no longer have to use the opinion box. If they have a smart phone or home computer they can access the network remotely or via the local wifi hotspot at each opinion box. Unless one wishes to use the opinion boxes unique features.

Each hotspot will facilitate 50 simultaneous video streams connections or 200 simultaneous content browsing connections. With fiber optic NBN backbone or clever mesh Wi-Fi programming, we could even facilitate a NON-METERED telco and web network.

Imagine That Freedom.

The operating system will evolve, from basic questions that are structured to be quantified, into a whole of community conversation organized into a coherent stream of relevant information, opinions and data.

Opinions Become Verse.

From here the community will co-define a new system for society which solves all the issues the community defines are issues by developing responsible plans.

The definition of Society I believe in is,

“A group of people joined by mutual consent to deliberate determine and act towards a common goal.”

Does Australian Society Reflect This Philosophy?

Do you believe in this philosophy?

If 70% of the community participate in the NEXT Great Conversation, via a network of 24 hour opinion boxes then this definition will be met very quickly.

Comment below if you want to host a community opinion box in front of your business or to integrate with your existing community notice board.
