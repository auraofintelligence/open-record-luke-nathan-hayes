Human Perspective Recording Tools Prize
Challenge:
Design an optimal human point of view perspective capturing tool or set of wearable smart device(s) such as contact lenses, sunglasses, head pieces or clothing.
The device(s) should capture high quality wide angle video and still images, as well as crisp clear audio. Also required is eye motion sensing linked to camera movement and focus, as well as voice control and sign language pattern recognition.
Consider technology research into clear-solid-state data storage and display materials, nano kinetic, magnetic, chemical and solar energy capture and recycling.
It is essential to collaborate with the groups who are designing Prize 1 solutions.

$100,000,000 AUD
