This 6 Week Challenge.
Become a facilitator in your Community and deliver the NEXT Great Conversation and create local jobs.
First step = Find 5 to 6 people to collaborate with,
then as a team follow all of the 6 week challenges.
By the end of the challenge, if you have successfully empowered the local community, you will each have
a full time or part time, paid role to continue your work to uplift local community living standards.
Week 1:
Evolve opt-in mobile number lists into fractals for SMS/MMS communication
Design, launch and manage contests to grow the list
Design, launch and evolve a series of “Aurasma Aura” campaigns
Research backpack bluetooth and wifi mobile broadcasting tools
Research the Australian festival scene to plan marketing and communication
Week 2:
Evolve opt-in mobile number lists for targeted SMS/MMS communication
Divide and split test all contest templates to target more diverse audiences
Divide and split test all Aurasma Auras to target more diverse audiences
Commence a crowdfunding campaign to purchase bluetooth+wifi backpacks
Follow your priority festival schedule to start negotiating with organizers
