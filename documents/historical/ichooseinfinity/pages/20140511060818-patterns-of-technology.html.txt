To Facilitate Infinity
On this page I will embed a series of videos about new technology which can facilitate a transformation of the impossible into the realms of possibility.
Imagine being able to communicate with computers via your thought patterns. Well it is possible right now, but it is still in the early development stages, and it requires millions of people just like you, to participate in the advancement of the #ideastream.
“Emotiv” is a low cost EEG head piece which reads your brainwaves, and can be programmed to send out your commands via wifi to nearby connected devices.
What would you like to be able to accomplish, just by thinking or talking out loud to computerized devices, people anywhere on the planet, or even a conscious digital entity which can understand your thoughts?
Together, could we solve every issue on the planet and facilitate an age of infinite sustainability, responsible abundance and participatory governance?
Click Here to go to Emotiv and view the cost of setting yourself up as a developer.
Now watch this next Awe Inspiring video and begin to wonder what comes next.
What if you could also control your game or computers with your eye movements? Tobii and SteelSeries have paved the way for this to occur and the system will be released to the consumer market in mid 2014. You can order your development kit NOW and define new ways to use the technology that fit into your infinite future. Take a look.
Click Here to go to Tobii and view the cost of setting yourself up as a developer.
Next I would like to show you how the #ideastream of Virtual Reality has been travelling in recent years. It has come a long way since the first people attempted to manifest a system of sensory immersion for a human being to visit a computerized digital dream world.
Oculus “Rift” is 2014′s cutting edge Virtual Reality Headset which is also still in its development stages. I believe it is worth further exploration by millions of people around the world. Are you interested?
Crowdsourcing Open Source software for this device is essential because it can facilitate solutions to some of the most challenging issues of NOW.
Imagine being able to put on a VR headset + an Emotiv to travel into a virtual model of the Universe. Within the model we could overlay all of the big data streams being captured about society, the environment, and our own selves. Click Here for Big Data on Climate.
What would you change if you could edit any part of the universe, at will, on any scale? How long would it take for a group of people to use the virtual model of our NOW reality, to solve all the issues of our reality?
Click Here to go to Oculus and view the cost of setting yourself up as a developer.
Next I would like you to think about Light as a mode of communication. Humanity now uses fibre optic cables to transmit immense amounts of data around the world at the speed of light. Well now we can also transmit data via Line of Sight LED Lights in a similar way as Wi-Fi (Video1) and via Laser Lights to more precise locations like the Moon (Video2)
Click Here to go to OLEDcomm and learn about their Commercially available Li-Fi Products
Click Here to go to NASA’s page on their Laser Communications Relay Demonstration
All humanity must begin to ask HOW can we use our minds together, to facilitate an infinite future for all life on Earth? It is Possible to make the Seemingly Impossible, Possible! One of the truest statements about human evolution is that Humans have always transcended our boundaries, environmental and man made, to make the impossible possible!
If you wish to join a community of people who strive daily to make the impossible possible, please connect. Together we will all co-define an infinite future.
