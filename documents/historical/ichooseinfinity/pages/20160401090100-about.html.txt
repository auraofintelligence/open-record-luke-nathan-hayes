Who is Luke Nathan: of the Hayes family?
I wish to do my full introductory interview with Kerry O’Brien, formerly of ABC’s “Four Corners”
Dear Reader,
I am Luke Nathan: of the Hayes family. Born in Redcliffe, Queensland Australia, on December 3rd 1982 to Kevin Thomas Hayes, a fitter and turner by trade, now the best automotive mechanic in Caboolture, and Sherelle Howard, a Registered Nurse and Health Information Systems Manager.
I have spent my most recent 6 years researching and connecting to global thought leaders and innovators in many fields. I did this to build my belief of an infinitely sustainable future, into a clear vision for actionable transition into such a reality as quickly and responsibly as possible. I achieved unwavering belief in this vision as of January 2015. I am a now bridge between realities and I seek to use digital game design and the internet of things to show everyone who dreams of a bright future, how amazing and infinitely abundant our world could become in 2, 5 and/or 10 years.
I have been studying game design in Brisbane, I commenced the diploma in April 2015 at Evocca College to learn how to communicate my vision to game and virtual reality developers and how to manage IT Projects. I am now at a level where I must start team building and seeking resources to move forward to connect with and lead the appropriate people to fill my skill gaps while doing more to replenish our planet and improve local living standards.
We can all do even better when we work together. I want your help and encouragement to bring me down from the dream to collaborate on the physical manifestation and co-creation of global responsible abundance. To do that, my weak points (money management, procrastination, stable home, appropriate workplace) must be supplemented by good people who can improve these areas of my life. I am currently only earning about $200/week from AUStudy and I do not come from an affluent background. I would prefer to live in a world of shared access to resources compared to the legislated fascination with ownership of things, corporations and people.
Let’s help everyone who wants help, by strategically working on tools and actions that lower or remove the limits of time, space, language, money, corruption, oppression, artificial scarcity and misinformation, while being as often as possible in the values lens of good conscience and joyous, virtuous, compassionate responsible abundance.
I have had many jobs in many industries. I have obtained many qualifications, though I have not completed a formal university degree. Most of my life I have specifically sought to travel under the radar, and to be mediocre despite my ability. I do not like the status quo and I love to question authority. Occasionally I have taken this distrust for authority too far, but I feel that who I am today is the most balanced and exceptional character that I have ever been.
I am single, heterosexual, polyamorous and I live in Spring Hill with 3 diversely different people. I love poetry, music and the ocean, in particular bodysurfing and bodyboarding. I choose to refrain from surfing and I have the scar around my left eye as a reminder. It is from an entanglement with a surfer’s board at Maroochydore some years ago.
If you want to know more, I am very approachable. I’d like to reinforce this as fact, as recently I read that having a beard in Australia makes you less approachable so I will dis-spell this in my case. Call me on 0434556652 if you would like to offer contributions towards any of the policies listed on http://ichooseinfinity.com http://alphainfinityfoundation.com or to request my attendance at an event. If you see me around town and are inspired to talk, please introduce yourself. My only request is that you approach me front on, and make friendly eye contact before attempting to shake hands or other physical contact like a hug.
Sincerely,
Luke Nathan: of the Hayes family
