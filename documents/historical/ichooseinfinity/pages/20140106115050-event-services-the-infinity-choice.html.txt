The Infinity Choice
Overview:
The Universe Is Evolving Us and We Are Evolving It. We must continue to learn and adapt to the Rapidly changing environment to survive, experience, understand and enjoy our lives.
The Point:
Infinity (symbol: ∞) is an abstract concept describing something without any limit and is relevant in a number of fields, predominantly mathematics and physics. The English word infinity derives from Latin infinitas, which can be translated as “unboundedness”, itself calqued from the Greek word apeiros, meaning “endless”.[1]
Question: Do you believe that all human beings have infinite creative potential?
The Infinity Choice:
To Choose to believe that you, along with every other human being, has infinite creative potential to create anything, achieve anything, experience anything, and live any story. Then to take that belief to the core of your being and move forward to co-create an infinite future full of love, cooperation, responsibility, respect, empathy and excitement.
I Choose Infinity ∞ Do You?
Pro’s and Con’s:
Pro: Your most exciting and inspiring existence has never been closer to becoming reality.
Con: Real change requires you accept the idea of a world future without money or poverty.
Decision Time:
How important to you, is a healthy environment, world peace, infinite opportunities, freedom, prosperity, family, good health, and time to relax? Choose Infinity and Co-Create Our Infinite Future. I Choose Infinity, Do You?
