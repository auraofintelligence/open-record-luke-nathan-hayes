What is a SelfConscious Perspective?
A SelfConscious Perspective describes a being imagining, remembering, or creating a perspective of their own self. It can also include a being imagining that they are another being, through into the infinite complexities of the other beings existence.
The following 2 presentations were created by Arthur Brock who I learned of on Monday the 9th of December 2013. Arthur has also been working towards whole system change for quite a few years and he has a lot of interesting ideas that people should be aware of.
In this second presentation the concepts of Society, Currency and Money are examined with some very interesting and perhaps surprising facts you should consider. If you like the next presentation then click this link to view http://www.metacurrency.org/
Stories and content on this page will be relevant to people who continually choose to become their best self and aim to learn from the experienced and imagined selfconscious perspectives of others.
