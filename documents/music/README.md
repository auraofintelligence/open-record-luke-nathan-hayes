## Published text and track order

The first two PDF collections follow the album track lists and lyric bodies in the [Music Universe catalogue at revision 14baf99](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/i-C-infinity-music-universe/blob/14baf99ddbc1fad52eaebaabc4e78f896b979293/data/catalogue.json). Wording, spelling, punctuation and section labels are retained. The reading editions add line wrapping, typography, page breaks and navigation. Their readable text companions preserve each published lyric body in a separate text block.

The title track of [Chronicles of the Forgotten](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/i-C-infinity-music-universe/albums/chronicles-of-the-forgotten/) occupies both [position 1](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/i-C-infinity-music-universe/songs/chronicles-of-the-forgotten-01-chronicles-of-the-forgotten/) and [position 16](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/i-C-infinity-music-universe/songs/chronicles-of-the-forgotten-16-chronicles-of-the-forgotten/) in that source. Both positions remain in the collected edition.

The supplied [third-album text](3rd%20Album%20Starseed%20Code%288%29.txt) retains its own ordering and wording. The fourth- and fifth-album PDF editions remain the unchanged files recorded in the [original inventory](../README.md#read-or-download). Public song pages provide the continuing catalogue alongside these dated documents.
