## 1. Purpose and Method

This document is a plain-speaking descriptive map, written in Australian English, of publicly available materials produced by Luke Nathan Hayes. It is intended as a starting point for an investigative journalist, long-form writer, or any reader who wants a single place to begin. No claims are advanced beyond what appears on the public pages or in the author’s own statements. Where a possible future development is discussed, it is labelled as a rational supposition grounded in the direction of the existing materials.

Primary materials are hosted at [auraofintelligence.github.io](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/) under a public-use licence with commercial rights reserved by the author. Anyone may read, cite, or discuss them. Commercial extraction or packaging requires separate agreement.

**Status note (author correction, October 2026):** The local Minjerribah / Redlands pages describe designed systems that are poised and awaiting conditions. They are not currently operating. [Strange but True](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/strange-but-true/) is registered; the author holds cards and receives income support, but has never operated a market stall. The only completed local project to date is a documentary and outdoor cinema evening produced for [MMEIC](../README.md#what-exists-and-what-is-still-proposed) marking 50 years of Deadly (NAIDOC 50th). Local community implementation has been deferred while the macro systems were built, because the author knows from experience (including as secretary of the [Point Lookout Fishing Club](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/PLFC_2026_Data/)) that when a committee does not pull its weight the work falls on one person, leading to burnout and withdrawal. The macro systems are intended to support clubs and businesses so that starting something does not recreate that load, and so the author can move beyond income support into a pattern of intermittent work, retirement, and travel with more resources and joyful responsible abundant opportunities.

## 3. Core Architecture Described in the Materials

The public materials describe interlocking ideas rather than a single finished product.

**[Aura of Intelligence](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/)** is presented as a personal mapping and computing environment intended to function as a body-and-mind digital twin. Technical descriptions refer to nested horn-torus geometry, memory palaces, pinning sequences, and pathways from Blender into Unity/VR. A [Genesis Protocol](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-genesis-geode-atlas/) is described as a multi-session sequence often linked to hyperbaric oxygen sessions.

**[C-Hour](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/) ([Community Hour](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/))** is a receipt for one verified hour of voluntary contribution. Consistent rules across materials: one hour equals one hour; earned through real human activity; non-speculative; kept separate from wages; each community chooses eligible activities, verification, and what access it unlocks.

**[Joyful Responsible Abundance](../README.md#the-purpose-connecting-the-projects)** is the directional frame used across submissions and plans, paired with the “[Protopian Gambit](../README.md#the-purpose-connecting-the-projects)”: a first move that improves something real, produces evidence, and leaves more choices open.

**[GAJRA Earth](https://gajra.earth/)** (Global Association for [Joyful Responsible Abundance](../README.md#the-purpose-connecting-the-projects) on Earth) is described as a voluntary association imaged as a flower garland—many distinct flowers connected by a shared thread, each keeping its own form. The original [GAJRA Earth website](https://gajra.earth/) preserves earlier fundraising, token and [Live Aid 2025 proposals](https://gajra.earth/about/). Related versions include the [early GitHub concept](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/GAJRA-earth/) ([GAJRA-earth repository](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/GAJRA-earth)), the [Luke × Claude build](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/gajra-earth-claude-build/) ([gajra-earth-claude-build repository](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/gajra-earth-claude-build)), the [Infinity build](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/GAJRA-earth-infinity/) ([GAJRA-earth-infinity repository](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/GAJRA-earth-infinity)) and the [public hub](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/gajra-earth-public-hub/) ([gajra-earth-public-hub repository](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/gajra-earth-public-hub)). Each version has its own contents and development history within the wider [GAJRA Earth](https://gajra.earth/) work.

**[Braided Economy](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/)** keeps three records distinct: ordinary money, a contribution record ([C-Hour](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/)), and a results record (savings, skills, resilience). The strands inform one another without being treated as the same thing.
