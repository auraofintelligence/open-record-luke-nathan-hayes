## Keep a usable reporting trail

Record the page or document title, author, URL, stated date or version, and your access date. For a changing GitHub file, use its history to obtain a permanent link to the commit examined. A file's upload date, its last edit date and the date of the event it describes may differ.

Use the originals when quoting; this guide paraphrases them. Separate the author's account, a demonstrated artefact, a proposal, a creative scenario and independent evidence. For health, scientific, legal or economic claims, locate the underlying studies or records and seek appropriate independent assessment; a design specification records an intention rather than a validated outcome.

Names of communities, organisations, public figures or potential partners in a proposal do not establish their participation or approval. In particular, Luke's work situated on Quandamooka Country should not be treated as an authorisation to speak for Traditional Owners or local organisations.
