## Childhood, family, making and performance

Luke was born in Redcliffe on **3 December 1982**. His recollection begins on roughly one hundred acres of mostly scrub forest at Ningi, where the family lived until he was about three or four. Hide-and-seek and tiggy were explorer games; Tiggy became the name of his first dog. The public map includes a childhood carpet-python incident, family births and deaths, learning to swim, changing homes and schools, and getting his first bodyboard and computer. These are parts of the life record alongside the later projects. Sources: [birth, `memory-3`](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L5); [F45, You blocks 2–5](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/luke-nathan-hayes-man-and-mind/blob/f040f5c259cd77f6377db2b7aaca840e36cae7c5/assets/archive/intake-20260906/f45.md#L111); [early memory entries](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L35).

His early interests included **keyboard, drama and singing**, as well as tennis, bodyboarding, technical drawing, woodwork and metalwork. The map names the Entertainment Academy for drama and singing, and records an early business washing cars and cleaning houses. Sources: [`memory-21`–`memory-23`](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L275); [singing, `memory-29`](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L350); [first business, `memory-37`](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L440).

Luke's corrected school record covers **Years 8–12 at Grace Lutheran College**. In his autobiographical account he says that he disliked the religious aspect but valued the tools and resources. He remembers trying not to stand out and doing the minimum in many subjects. The [scanned 1999 Senior Certificate](https://lukecatalyst.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Lukes-Certs-and-Licences.pdf#page=3) confirms High Achievement in English and Biological Science and an **A on the QCS test**. His remembered OP in the autobiographical answer is qualified with “I think”; the separate tertiary entrance statement is on page 4 of the evidence PDF. Sources: [corrected school entry](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Lukes-world-of-work-experience/blob/2a672fdd2c78fb220647b0a25876838d121280a3/public/content/resume.json#L1474); [F45, You block 11](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/luke-nathan-hayes-man-and-mind/blob/f040f5c259cd77f6377db2b7aaca840e36cae7c5/assets/archive/intake-20260906/f45.md#L239).

The same public evidence pack contains **Certificate II in Automotive (Mechanical–Underbody), AUR21699**, awarded by Brisbane North Institute of TAFE on **3 September 2003** ([page 5](https://lukecatalyst.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Lukes-Certs-and-Licences.pdf#page=5)), and **Certificate IV in Business (Small Business Management), BSB40401**, awarded by Sarina Russo on **23 June 2009** ([page 22](https://lukecatalyst.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Lukes-Certs-and-Licences.pdf#page=22)). The corrected business-study entry dates study to 2008; the scanned certificate supplies the later award date. The [main account's evidence table](../README.md#education-practical-work-and-volunteering) also identifies references, the 2004 Glastonbury crew pass and later training.

At the month-long Googa outdoor camp in Year 10, he recalls winning the boys' orienteering division by a substantial margin despite not being one of the fastest runners. Another remembered sporting moment is coming back from 5–1 down and match points against him in an under-15 tennis match, followed by the team's championship win. His account includes the wish to impress his coach as well as team loyalty, ego and competitive determination. Sources: [F45, You block 13](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/luke-nathan-hayes-man-and-mind/blob/f040f5c259cd77f6377db2b7aaca840e36cae7c5/assets/archive/intake-20260906/f45.md#L273); [You blocks 18–20](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/luke-nathan-hayes-man-and-mind/blob/f040f5c259cd77f6377db2b7aaca840e36cae7c5/assets/archive/intake-20260906/f45.md#L400).

He entered science studies at the University of the Sunshine Coast, left lectures to bodyboard and quit during the first term. His account of early adulthood includes moving in with a school friend, a silver 1981 Holden Gemini, supermarket work, house parties, nightclubs, girlfriends and a beach-going life. He then spent six months in Maroochydore and six in Mooloolaba before returning to Caboolture to work with his father. [F45, You block 11](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/luke-nathan-hayes-man-and-mind/blob/f040f5c259cd77f6377db2b7aaca840e36cae7c5/assets/archive/intake-20260906/f45.md#L239) gives this sequence directly.

## A working life across different places

The work history records a first job stocking drinks fridges and cleaning toilets at **Mobil Burpengary**, followed by Woolworths customer service and perishables work. In his father's automotive business, Luke learned servicing, brakes, steering, suspension, wheel alignment and balancing through a roughly two-and-a-half-year traineeship. The corrected record separately supplies the employment dates January 2001 to January 2004 and his 2003 automotive underbody study. [Work records and source notes](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Lukes-world-of-work-experience/blob/2a672fdd2c78fb220647b0a25876838d121280a3/public/content/resume.json#L832) preserve those distinctions.

The UK working holiday brought temporary bar work, office demolition at Tower 42 and gift-card factory work, followed by a festival season with **W.A.A.P. Wing and a Prayer Event Services** in 2004. Other assignments included installing storage marquees at supermarket sites and temporary horse stables. After returning to Australia he worked in roofing, event marquees and shopfitting, and as a Queensland Rail apprentice traction powerlinesman. Sources: [UK and later practical work records](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Lukes-world-of-work-experience/blob/2a672fdd2c78fb220647b0a25876838d121280a3/public/content/resume.json#L948).

### Northern Australia

Luke lived in Palmerston, Nightcliff and Darwin. His work there included assisting a semi-trailer mechanic; ground crew and truck driving for **ACE Treelopping Services**; and skilled labour welding precast concrete culverts at **Hume Concrete**. At **Skystar Airport Services**, Luke worked on check-in, boarding, customs liaison and airside services for Jetstar's Cairns–Darwin–Singapore route. The corrected airport employment dates are July 2006 to June 2007. Sources: [memory-map northern chapter](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L1220); [residence entries `home-80`–`home-83`](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L3961); [airport, trades and source records](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Lukes-world-of-work-experience/blob/2a672fdd2c78fb220647b0a25876838d121280a3/public/content/resume.json#L1164).

The next working landscape included Mackay and Bowen Basin coal-mine shutdowns with **Ausblast Industries**, recorded from July 2007 to June 2008. Luke's memory map also includes confined-space and height training, BMA licences and work across as many as ten mine sites. Sources: [`ausblast`](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Lukes-world-of-work-experience/blob/2a672fdd2c78fb220647b0a25876838d121280a3/public/content/resume.json#L1247); [`memory-121`–`memory-124`](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L1460).

Luke taught himself web design in **2008**, studied small-business management through Sarina Russo/NEIS and established **Creating Attraction**. The map also records neuro-linguistic programming, entrepreneurship learning, Community Leverage and MyRecovery. The personal site's account of 2008–2012 reports a podcast series reaching **more than 80,000 listens**. The memory map explicitly confirms three active periods for **The Next Great Conversation / iChooseInfinity: 2013–2018, 2020 and 2021–2023**. The directly corrected dates are January–March 2020, February–May 2022 and September–December 2022 respectively. ## More than forty moves, money and relationships

Luke says he makes friends easily but has sometimes left relationships untended when his interest changed or he moved away. He describes more than forty house moves and repeated starts in new places. The map preserves repeat residence entries, including returns to Amity, Brisbane and Delhi, while leaving the dates of those residences open. Sources: [F45, You block 12](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/luke-nathan-hayes-man-and-mind/blob/f040f5c259cd77f6377db2b7aaca840e36cae7c5/assets/archive/intake-20260906/f45.md#L259); [residence list](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L3870).

His financial aim is direct. He describes being tired of poverty, having worked for hourly wages and having lacked a business mentor. He connects lack of money with difficulty cultivating the relationships and experiences he wants. In a later answer he says he plans never again to need to ask for money or exchange his time for wages. These intentions help explain Mutual Futures, shared ownership, intermittent retirement and a system intended to support his own chosen life as well as other people's. Sources: [You blocks 12–14](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/luke-nathan-hayes-man-and-mind/blob/f040f5c259cd77f6377db2b7aaca840e36cae7c5/assets/archive/intake-20260906/f45.md#L259).

The public map also retains breakups, family changes, illness and injuries. Entries include an elbow injury associated with Americold, a knee injury and a separate undated list of illnesses, operations and accidents. Their presence does not tell the whole story of any job ending or move; the map leaves many of those connections unwritten. Sources: [`memory-249`](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L3412); [`memory-260`](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L3547); [illness and injury fragments](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/lifetime-memory-map/blob/472251c87230b31b7f83fec73202a5a38caa0a46/data/memories.json#L4511).
