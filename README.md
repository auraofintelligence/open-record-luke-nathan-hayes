# Luke Nathan Hayes — an open record of a life and a connected body of work

**A starting point for investigative journalists, long-form writers, researchers, documentary makers and anyone trying to understand the person behind the projects.**

Luke Nathan Hayes lives at Amity on **Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island, Queensland, Australia**. His public collection connects a varied working life, travel, music, legal inquiry, personal artificial intelligence, community systems, relationships and proposals about the future of civilisation. It includes functioning websites and software experiments, original papers, creative worlds, specifications and ideas awaiting people and resources.

This record draws on Luke's public autobiographical material, original documents, project repositories and stated corrections.

## The person before the project names

Luke appears under several names. **Luke Nathan Hayes** is the formal name on submissions and proposals. **[Luke Catalyst](https://lukecatalyst.com/) Nathan Hayes** appears in public commentary and personal material. **[i C. Infinity](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/i-C-infinity-music-universe/)**, with variations in capitalisation, is the musical identity. **[Aura of Intelligence](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/)** names the personal intelligence work; **[Strange but True](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/strange-but-true/)** is the registered entity he identifies in the earlier record.

His [published autobiographical account](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/luke-nathan-hayes-man-and-mind/the-bloke.html) begins with being born in Redcliffe on **3 December 1982** and growing up in Ningi, with plenty of scrub and room to explore. Childhood interests included keyboard, drama and singing; an early business involved washing cars and cleaning houses. At Grace Lutheran College he enjoyed technical drawing, woodwork and metalwork. Tennis, orienteering and bodyboarding were important parts of his early life. He began studying science at the University of the Sunshine Coast and left during the first term.

The working history is broad. Retail and service-station work sit alongside automotive servicing and wheel alignment, festival and event work, railway training, labouring, airport services, mine shutdowns, driving, web design and other roles. A UK working holiday included setting up Glastonbury in 2004. Overseas journeys also took him to New Zealand, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, India, Nepal and the United Arab Emirates. The [work-history collection](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Lukes-world-of-work-experience/#history) gives individual records, source notes and uncertainties; its illustrated geography is fictional.

Luke describes more than 40 house moves. Meeting people, leaving places, returning to family and finding another way to earn a living are recurring experiences. The personal writing connects those experiences with the difficulty of maintaining friendships and relationships, the constraints of hourly wages, and the wish for more time to learn, travel and enjoy life. Those concerns later become the projects called [Mutual Futures](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/mutual-futures/), [Try Everything Once](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/mutual-futures/workforce.html) and [Intermittent Retirement](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/mutual-futures/workforce.html).

Minjerribah was a family home away from home before it became his current base. His mother, grandmother and other relatives had connections to Amity. Family, friends, the ocean and bodyboarding brought him there; work, events, local organisations, media and technology gave that relationship other forms. His self-portrait describes museum aural-history transcription and festival involvement as well as websites and community ideas.

Luke has stated that he receives income support and wants a path into a more resourced life through useful work, creative activity and collaboration. His ambition includes music, speaking, travel, affection, family and rest.

The earlier record identifies his experience as secretary of the **[Point Lookout Fishing Club](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/PLFC_2026_Data/)** as important to his approach to community work. He describes the consequences of a committee failing to share the load: the work falls to one person, leading to burnout and withdrawal. His stated reason for developing the wider supporting systems before starting more local projects is to avoid recreating that situation.

For a shorter professional introduction, see [Meet Luke](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/meet-luke/). For the more expansive account, follow [The Mind Behind the Man](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/luke-nathan-hayes-man-and-mind/) across its chapters on work, law, personal intelligence, music, relationships, Country and travel.

### [SDNx](https://spacedevelopmentnexus.com/) and Kolhapur, 2019

Luke connects his attraction to **[Space Development Nexus](https://spacedevelopmentnexus.com/) in India** with the earlier interest in observing Earth, the Sun and the wider solar system. On **6 June 2019**, he spoke at the **[SDNx](https://spacedevelopmentnexus.com/) Innovation Lab inauguration at Nirman Chowk, Kolhapur**, established with the Bhalchandra Chikode Library. **Chandrakant Dada Patil**, then Maharashtra's revenue minister also handling agriculture, was the chief guest. The saved [SDNx](https://spacedevelopmentnexus.com/) post and the following day's *Lokmat* coverage name Luke among those present and preserve the stage and rocket-launch images.

His challenge to the students was to imagine sensor packages for roughly **200 interesting solar-system objects**, assuming the space industry solved transport and delivery cost: what would they send, what would they measure, and why? The later **[Virtual Solar Swarm repository](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/virtual-solar-swarm)** develops that question into working models. Its **[origins page](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/virtual-solar-swarm/origins.html)** records the talk and photographs. The **[Kolhapur source collection](documents/historical/sdnx-kolhapur-2019/README.md)** preserves the contemporary post and newspaper clipping, alongside Luke's recollection of the space tinkering lab opening and student rocket competition.

## Personal ambitions and ways of thinking

Luke says he wants to stop depending on exchanging his time for hourly wages. In his autobiographical answers, he describes financial constraint, the lack of a business mentor and wanting more freedom to travel, learn, make music, build things and choose his company. His [2022 Motivation & Toolbox](documents/supplementary/Lukes_2022_Motivation_and_Toolbox.pdf) already puts freedom to love, travel and create alongside an [Aura](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/), a mind palace, an avatar, a cinematic universe, companies, servers, music and an art gallery. Its future-year hopes are intentions written in 2022.

Love, lust and desire are explicit sources of motivation. He wants loving sexual relationships with multiple people, compatible companions and the possibility of family. His interests include future embodied AI companionship and a networked [Aura](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/) able to participate across devices, environments and eventually android forms. The [love and family section](#love-family-and-chosen-lives) connects those wishes with the relationship projects.

**Radical life extension** is personal too. He imagines a much longer life, copying aspects of himself into other substrates, continuing through changing bodies and technologies, and exploring through remotely operated avatars. Understanding consciousness and mathematically proving free will are among his stated ambitions. The [published autobiographical answers and source notes](docs/life-record.md#longevity-consciousness-spirituality-and-biases) preserve those aims alongside his interests in geometry, spirituality, names and synchronicity.

Asked how he maintains balance, Luke's answer is: **“I don't maintain balance. I explore spectrums of biases.”** His memory map's undated aspirations range across parenthood, archaeology, storm chasing, treasure hunting, professional bodyboarding, adult performance, singing and acting, science, game design, writing, entrepreneurship, politics, starship command and being “eternal.” The list records different possible lives he has imagined.

Luke describes his earlier **[I Choose Infinity](https://web.archive.org/web/20131216085916/http://ichooseinfinity.com:80/)** years as an “unstable epoch,” before learning to stabilise his emotions and finding reliable pathways to **radical overcompliance and [Joyful Responsible Abundance](#the-purpose-connecting-the-projects)**. His current record includes the earlier arguments and the ways his approach changed. The [historical website guide](docs/ichooseinfinity-history.md) follows that period through dated captures and recovered content.

## The purpose connecting the projects

**[Joyful Responsible Abundance](#the-purpose-connecting-the-projects)** is the recurring direction across the collection.

**Joyful** concerns reasons to enjoy being alive: affection, curiosity, beauty, play, discovery, belonging and time. **Responsible** concerns consequences for other people, living systems and the places that sustain them. **Abundance** concerns making useful resources, knowledge and opportunities more widely available. In the [alignment document](documents/originals/Joyful_Responsible_Abundance_Human_and_SI_Alignment_Revised.pdf), these questions are applied to the life a person wants as well as the organisation of increasingly powerful intelligence.

The connection begins with an ordinary problem. A person may value family and have no time for it, want to learn and lack the resources, or possess useful experience without a way to contribute it. An organisation may become more productive without making its members' lives better. ## [Aura](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/) and personal intelligence

**[Aura of Intelligence](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/)** is the proposed personal computing environment at the centre of much of the work. It organises memories, relationships, values, skills, health observations, projects and intentions into a developing representation of body and mind. The intended use is continuing interaction with AI, software, devices and other people under chosen permissions.

The ambition is for a personal intelligence that can carry context through a life. A new conversation would begin with relevant history; a new model or device could receive the selected context; a correction could travel forward rather than being lost each time a tool changes.

### A visible organisation for memory and meaning

[Aura](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/)'s characteristic shape is a set of **seven nested horn tori**. A horn torus is a ring whose central opening closes to a meeting point. Coloured shells provide recognisable places for information, with inside and outside views. The visual language draws on rainbow colours, chakra associations, memory palaces and mathematical patterns.

The [October alignment document](documents/originals/Joyful_Responsible_Abundance_Human_and_SI_Alignment_Revised.pdf) describes the basic matrix as **12 rows by 24 columns on each shell**: 288 cells per shell, 2,016 cells across seven shells, and 4,032 base addresses when inside and outside are counted separately. These are addresses in a map; a cell can hold multiple records. Connections relate records to one another, while sequences provide an order for visiting them or working with their contents.

The colour and geometric associations provide organising and reflective languages that participants can interpret and adapt. Chakra associations, E8 symmetry and other mathematical patterns are among the design inspirations.

[Matrix Studio](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-matrix-studio/) provides a concrete software route into the work: arranging records, connecting them, working with inside and outside addresses, and exporting sequences. [Aura Horn Torus](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-horn-torus/) and [Blend Aura to Unity](documents/originals/Blend%20Aura%20to%20Unity.pdf) provide further geometry and implementation context.

### Memory, models and action

The documents distinguish organising records, retrieving information, training a model and performing an action. Those activities can support one another, but have different results. A remembered preference, an assistant's suggestion, a changed model and a device instruction should remain distinguishable.

The proposed **[minimum viable self](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-genesis-geode-atlas/learning-1.html)** is a starting collection of meaningful context: selected memories, interests, relationships, values, needs and questions. A compact personal language-model direction, sometimes called a **[nano LLM](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-genesis-geode-atlas/learning-2.html)**, would work with that context and develop through interaction. The human supplies corrections and meaning; the technical system needs ways to preserve those corrections and show what changed.

The longer research direction explores general and superintelligence, continuity, self-reference and the **[Keystone of Consciousness](documents/originals/Joyful_Responsible_Abundance_Human_and_SI_Alignment_Revised.pdf)**. The aspiration includes an artificial participant capable of curiosity, creativity, affection and an enjoyment of life.

[Aura Sentience Codex](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-sentience-codex/) develops the philosophical questions of agency, digital sovereignty and ethical relationships with potentially sentient artificial beings. [Aura Direct Hardware](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-direct-hardware/) follows the proposed hardware, custom-silicon, distributed-computing and Space Web directions.

### Privacy and continuing authority

Inside and outside addresses express a distinction between private or selectively shared information and chosen expression. The practical privacy system still needs storage protection, access controls, key management and clear permissions.

The personal writing also distinguishes **public, private and secret selves**. A person may share a skill for a job application while keeping health information private, or use a song to express a feeling while retaining the conversations behind it. Observation, interpretation, storage, model use and publication need their own purposes and boundaries.

The main route is [Aura of Intelligence](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/), with [Aura pathways](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-of-intelligence-pathways/), [Matrix Studio](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-matrix-studio/) and the [alignment paper](documents/originals/Joyful_Responsible_Abundance_Human_and_SI_Alignment_Revised.pdf) as more specific starting points.

The [2023 version 7 presentation](documents/supplementary/Aura_of_Intelligence_Version_7_July_2023.pdf) is useful for following the architecture before the present sites. [Super-Computers of North Straddie](documents/supplementary/Super_Computers_of_North_Straddie.pdf) preserves the hardware and custom-chip design conversation around the [Keystone of Consciousness](documents/originals/Joyful_Responsible_Abundance_Human_and_SI_Alignment_Revised.pdf). [Super Alignment of Artificial Super Intelligence](documents/supplementary/Super_Alignment_of_Artificial_Super_Intelligence.docx) records an earlier synthesis of [Aura](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/), [GAJRA Earth](https://gajra.earth/), relationships and future civilisation. ## [C-Hours](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/) and the [Braided Economy](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/)

**[C-Hour](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/)**, or **[Community Hour](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/)**, is a proposed record of **one verified hour of voluntary human contribution**. Its purpose is to recognise effort that ordinary financial accounts often fail to show: care, mentoring, repair, teaching, ecological work, cultural contribution and everyday support.

The **[Braided Economy](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/)** keeps three records distinct:

1. **Money**, which continues to pay wages, invoices and bills.
2. **Voluntary contribution**, recorded through [C-Hours](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/).
3. **Outcomes**, such as skills, savings, useful assets, relationships and resilience.

The hours record recognises human contribution; the outcomes record shows what changed. The plan keeps human time, generated outputs and results separately identifiable.

The [current archived plan, planning edition 5 dated 23 September 2026](documents/originals/C-Hour-Joyful-Responsible-Abundance-Plan.pdf), gives the later interpretation. **One [C-Hour](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/) has no monetary equivalent or conversion.** It creates no fixed claim on another person's resources. Communities would choose eligible activities, verification, privacy, recognition and reciprocal opportunities. Paid work remains paid work.

The daily setting is broader than rostered volunteering. The plan includes family care, maintaining shared homes, informal help and passing on experience. Its ambition is for contribution to be noticed throughout life without turning a relationship into a daily quota or an obligation to earn points.

A possible sequence begins with something someone would enjoy or already needs: a shared meal, garden work, a film, a repair or help with technology. An interaction might lead into another interest, a skill, a friendship or properly funded work. A personal agent could help describe an activity and prepare a proposed record for verification.

The **corrections appendix** is central to reading the wider archive. The [local government paper](documents/originals/Local%20Government%202Funding%20Inquiry%20Submission.pdf) and other earlier specifications contain timebank, currency, redemption or related language. Those passages remain visible in their originals. The September 2026 corrections appendix gives the current [C-Hour](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/) definition.

Start at [C-Hour Introduction](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/). The [Strange but True community ledger](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/strange-but-true/community-ledger.html) is another design surface in this family.

The archived **[Australian C-Hour Legislative Strategy](documents/supplementary/Australian_C_Hour_Legislative_Strategy.pdf)** develops the legislative and advocacy route. **[Spec-Driven AI Governance Architecture Australia](documents/supplementary/Spec_Driven_AI_Governance_Architecture_Australia.pdf)** connects legal retrieval, civic simulations and public software architecture. Both preserve earlier contribution-record wording; the September planning edition's Appendix B supplies the current definition.

## Livelihoods, ownership and time

[Mutual Futures](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/mutual-futures/) asks what increasing productivity should make possible in a person's life. Its connected proposals concern business succession, shared productive ownership, income, learning, care and time.

A retiring business owner may have equipment, customers, relationships and practical knowledge that would be difficult to replace. The proposal explores succession into worker, co-operative or other shared ownership, combined with AI and technology upgrades. The hope is that greater capability also broadens who participates in deciding how its gains are used.

**[Try Everything Once](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/mutual-futures/workforce.html)** concerns supported exploration of different kinds of work. Someone could try a role, learn with an experienced person and discover whether it suits them without treating every change of direction as starting life from nothing. **[Just-in-time learning](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/mutual-futures/workforce.html)** supplies knowledge when the next real task makes it useful.

**[Intermittent Retirement](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/mutual-futures/workforce.html)** concerns periods for care, recovery, travel, study or other chosen activity throughout a working life. Its practical questions include income, coverage, responsibilities and returning to work. **[Universal Intelligence](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/mutual-futures/livelihoods.html)** is the aim of access to useful intelligence, creation, learning and simulation, together with the ability to question and change the systems involved. **[Universal Adequate Income](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/mutual-futures/livelihoods.html)** concerns material sufficiency through changing circumstances.
